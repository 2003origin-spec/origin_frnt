## Reproductive health and population

:::question{title="1. WHO definition" exam="NEET level"}
According to WHO, reproductive health is total well-being in which aspects of reproduction?

---

**Physical, emotional, behavioural and social.** It is not limited to healthy organs.
:::

:::question{title="2. Amniocentesis" exam="NEET level"}
Which statement about amniocentesis is correct?
(a) It is banned in India for all uses.
(b) It tests foetal cells in amniotic fluid for genetic disorders.
(c) It is a method of contraception.
(d) It is used to transfer embryos.

---

**(b).** Only its use for sex-determination is banned. It still helps detect disorders like Down syndrome.
:::

## Contraception

:::question{title="3. Hormone IUD" exam="NEET level"}
Which of these is a hormone releasing IUD: Lippes loop, Cu7, Multiload 375, LNG-20?

---

**LNG-20.** Lippes loop is non-medicated; Cu7 and Multiload 375 release copper.
:::

:::question{title="4. Saheli" exam="NEET level"}
Give two ways in which Saheli differs from the usual oral pills.

---

Saheli is **non-steroidal** and is taken **once a week**. Usual pills contain progestogens (with or without estrogen) and are taken daily for 21 days.
:::

:::question{title="5. Match the method" exam="NEET level"}
Match:
A. Lactational amenorrhea, B. Cervical cap, C. CuT, D. Vasectomy
with
(i) Surgical, (ii) Natural, (iii) Barrier, (iv) IUD.

---

**A–(ii), B–(iii), C–(iv), D–(i).**
:::

:::question{title="6. Statements on contraceptives" exam="NEET level"}
Which statements are correct?
I. Emergency contraception works if used within 72 hours of coitus.
II. Tubectomy is done through an incision on the scrotum.
III. Condoms also protect against STIs.
IV. Diaphragms are disposable.

---

**I and III only.** Tubectomy is done in the female (incision in the abdomen or through the vagina). Diaphragms are reusable.
:::

## MTP and STIs

:::question{title="7. Safe MTP" exam="NEET level"}
Up to which point of pregnancy is MTP considered relatively safe?

---

**The first trimester, i.e. up to 12 weeks.** Second trimester abortions are much riskier.
:::

:::question{title="8. Curable STIs" exam="NEET level"}
Assertion (A): Gonorrhoea and syphilis are completely curable if detected early.
Reason (R): All STIs, including HIV and hepatitis-B, are completely curable.
(a) Both true, R explains A (b) Both true, R does not explain A (c) A true, R false (d) Both false

---

**(c).** A is true. R is false: hepatitis-B, genital herpes and HIV are not completely curable.
:::

## Infertility and ART

:::question{title="9. Where does the embryo go?" exam="NEET level"}
An embryo with 16 blastomeres is formed by IVF. Which technique transfers it, and to which organ?

---

**IUT (intra uterine transfer), into the uterus.** Only embryos with up to 8 blastomeres go into the fallopian tube (ZIFT).
:::

:::question{title="10. Choose the technique" exam="NEET level"}
A woman cannot produce ova, but her fallopian tubes and uterus can support fertilisation and development. Which technique places a donor ovum in her fallopian tube?

---

**GIFT (gamete intra fallopian transfer).** Fertilisation then happens inside her body.
:::
