## Key terms

:::formula{title="Reproductive health (WHO)"}
A total well-being in all aspects of reproduction: **physical, emotional, behavioural and social**.
:::

:::formula{title="Programmes in India"}
- **Family planning:** started in **1951**.
- Now called **Reproductive and Child Health Care (RCH) programmes**.
:::

:::formula{title="Infertility"}
Inability to conceive or produce children even after **2 years** of unprotected sexual cohabitation.
:::

## Contraceptive methods

| Type | Examples | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Natural | Periodic abstinence, withdrawal (coitus interruptus), lactational amenorrhea | Ovum and sperm don't meet |
| Barrier | Condoms (Nirodh), diaphragms, cervical caps, vaults | Physical barrier |
| IUDs | Lippes loop, CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375, Progestasert, LNG-20 | See below |
| Oral pills | Progestogen or progestogen–estrogen; Saheli | Inhibit ovulation and implantation; alter cervical mucus |
| Injectables, implants | Progestogens ± estrogen | Like pills, last longer |
| Surgical | Vasectomy, tubectomy | Block gamete transport |

## IUDs in detail

| Kind | Examples | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Non-medicated | Lippes loop | More phagocytosis of sperms (all IUDs do this) |
| Copper releasing | CuT, Cu7, Multiload 375 | Cu ions lower sperm motility and fertilising capacity |
| Hormone releasing | Progestasert, LNG-20 | Uterus unsuitable for implantation, cervix hostile to sperms |

| --- | --- |
| Common STIs (NCERT list) | Gonorrhoea, syphilis, genital herpes, chlamydiasis, genital warts, trichomoniasis, hepatitis-B, HIV (AIDS) |

## Assisted reproductive technologies (ART)

| Short form | Full form | What is transferred, and where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| IVF–ET | In vitro fertilisation – embryo transfer | "Test tube baby" programme |
| ZIFT | Zygote intra fallopian transfer | Zygote or embryo up to 8 blastomeres, into the fallopian tube |
| IUT | Intra uterine transfer | Embryo with more than 8 blastomeres, into the uterus |
| GIFT | Gamete intra fallopian transfer | Donor ovum, into the fallopian tube |
| ICSI | Intra cytoplasmic sperm injection | Sperm injected straight into the ovum (in the lab) |
| AI / IUI | Artificial insemination / intra-uterine insemination | Semen (husband or donor), into the vagina or uterus |
