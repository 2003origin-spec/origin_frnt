## Double helix numbers

:::formula{title="Chargaff's rule (double-stranded DNA)"}
$$\frac{A}{T} = \frac{G}{C} = 1$$
So A = T and G = C, and purines = pyrimidines ($A + G = T + C$).

If C = 20%, then G = 20%, A + T = 60%, so **A = 30%**.
:::

:::formula{title="Helix measurements"}
- Pitch (one full turn): **3.4 nm**
- Base pairs per turn: about **10**
- Distance between two base pairs: about **0.34 nm**
- Coiling: **right-handed**; strands **anti-parallel**
:::

:::formula{title="Hydrogen bonds"}
A = T: **2** H-bonds. G ≡ C: **3** H-bonds.

$$\text{H-bonds} = 2(\text{A–T pairs}) + 3(\text{G–C pairs})$$
:::

:::formula{title="Length of DNA"}
$$\text{length} = \text{bp} \times 0.34 \times 10^{-9}\ \text{m}$$
Mammalian cell: $6.6 \times 10^9 \times 0.34 \times 10^{-9} \approx$ **2.2 m**.

*E. coli* DNA of 1.36 mm has about $4 \times 10^6$ bp.
:::

## Nucleosides and nucleotides

| Part | DNA | RNA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sugar | Deoxyribose | Ribose (extra 2'-OH) |
| Purines | A, G | A, G |
| Pyrimidines | C, T | C, U |

- **Nucleoside** = base + sugar (N-glycosidic bond at 1'C). Example: adenosine, deoxyadenosine.
- **Nucleotide** = nucleoside + phosphate (phosphoester bond at 5'C).
- Nucleotides join by **3'–5' phosphodiester** bonds.

## DNA fingerprinting

- Probe: **VNTR** (Variable Number of Tandem Repeats), a **mini-satellite**.
- VNTR size: **0.1 to 20 kb**.
- Polymorphism: a variant at a locus with frequency **greater than 0.01** in the population.
