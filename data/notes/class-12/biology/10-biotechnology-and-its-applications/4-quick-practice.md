## Agriculture

:::question{title="1. Banana from a sick plant" exam="NEET level"}
A banana plant is infected with a virus. A farmer wants healthy plants of the same variety. Which part should be cultured?

---

**The meristem** (apical or axillary). It is free of virus even in an infected plant, so it gives virus-free plants.
:::

:::question{title="2. Identical plantlets" exam="NEET level"}
Thousands of plantlets are raised from one explant of an apple plant. What are these plantlets called?

---

**Somaclones.** They are genetically identical to the plant they came from.
:::

:::question{title="3. Bt toxin activation" exam="NEET level"}
Which condition turns Bt protoxin into active toxin inside an insect?

(a) Acidic pH of the stomach (b) Alkaline pH of the gut (c) Bacterial enzymes (d) Sunlight on the leaf

---

**(b) Alkaline pH of the gut.** It dissolves the crystals, and the active toxin then makes pores in the midgut epithelial cells.
:::

:::question{title="4. Match the cry genes" exam="NEET level"}
Match the gene with its target.

A. *cryIAb* B. *cryIAc* C. *cryIIAb*

(i) Cotton bollworm (ii) Corn borer

---

**A–(ii), B–(i), C–(i).** *cryIAc* and *cryIIAb* control cotton bollworms; *cryIAb* controls corn borer.
:::

:::question{title="5. RNAi in tobacco" exam="NEET level"}
**Assertion (A):** Transgenic tobacco that makes both sense and anti-sense RNA of a nematode gene resists the nematode.

**Reason (R):** The two RNAs form dsRNA, which silences the matching mRNA of the nematode.

(a) Both true, R explains A (b) Both true, R does not explain A (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true

---

**(a).** The dsRNA starts RNAi, the nematode's mRNA is silenced, and the parasite cannot survive.
:::

## Medicine

:::question{title="6. Missing piece" exam="NEET level"}
Which part of pro-insulin is absent from mature insulin?

---

**The C peptide.** It is removed during maturation; mature insulin has only chains A and B joined by disulphide bridges.
:::

:::question{title="7. Statements on ADA therapy" exam="NEET level"}
Which statement about gene therapy for ADA deficiency is **wrong**?

(a) The functional ADA cDNA is carried by a retroviral vector.
(b) The patient's lymphocytes are grown outside the body.
(c) One infusion of engineered lymphocytes cures the patient for life.
(d) ADA deficiency affects the immune system.

---

**(c)** is wrong. The lymphocytes are not immortal, so infusions must be repeated.
:::

:::question{title="8. Early detection" exam="NEET level"}
A doctor wants to detect a virus in a patient before any symptoms appear, while the virus is present in very small numbers. Which technique suits best?

---

**PCR.** It amplifies the virus's nucleic acid, so even very low amounts can be detected.
:::

## Transgenic animals and ethics

:::question{title="9. Rosie" exam="NEET level"}
Which human protein did the milk of Rosie, the transgenic cow, contain?

(a) α-1-antitrypsin (b) Insulin (c) Alpha-lactalbumin (d) ADA

---

**(c) Alpha-lactalbumin**, at 2.4 g per litre. α-1-antitrypsin is the protein used to treat emphysema.
:::

:::question{title="10. Which body?" exam="NEET level"}
Which Indian body decides on the validity of GM research and the safety of releasing GM organisms for public services?

---

**GEAC** (Genetic Engineering Approval Committee).
:::
