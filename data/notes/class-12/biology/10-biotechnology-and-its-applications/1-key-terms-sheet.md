## The big picture

:::formula{title="Three critical research areas of biotechnology"}
1. The best **catalyst**: an improved organism (usually a microbe) or a pure enzyme.
2. **Optimal conditions**, made by engineering, for the catalyst to act.
3. **Downstream processing**: purifying the protein or organic compound.
:::

:::formula{title="Three options to increase food production"}
1. Agro-chemical based agriculture
2. Organic agriculture
3. Genetically engineered crop-based agriculture
:::

## Plant tissue culture terms

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Explant | Any plant part grown in a test tube |
| Totipotency | Power of any cell or explant to form a whole plant |
| Micro-propagation | Making thousands of plants by tissue culture |
| Somaclones | Plants genetically identical to the parent |
| Protoplast | A plant cell without its cell wall |
| Somatic hybrid | Plant grown from two fused protoplasts |

:::formula{title="Tissue culture medium"}
- A **carbon source**, such as sucrose
- Inorganic salts, vitamins, amino acids
- Growth regulators: **auxins, cytokinins**, etc.
:::

## Bt crops

:::formula{title="Bt toxin"}
- Made by the bacterium ***Bacillus thuringiensis*** (Bt).
- Stored as **inactive protoxin** crystals.
- **Alkaline pH** of the insect gut dissolves the crystals and activates the toxin.
- Active toxin binds **midgut epithelial cells**, makes pores, and the cells swell and burst (lysis). The insect dies.
:::

| Insect group | NCERT examples |
| --- | --- |
| Lepidopterans | Tobacco budworm, armyworm |
| Coleopterans | Beetles |
| Dipterans | Flies, mosquitoes |

| Gene | Pest it controls |
| --- | --- |
| *cryIAc* | Cotton bollworms |
| *cryIIAb* | Cotton bollworms |
| *cryIAb* | Corn borer |

## Nematode-resistant tobacco (RNAi)

:::formula{title="RNA interference at a glance"}
- Pest: nematode *Meloidegyne incognitia* (NCERT spelling; usually *Meloidogyne incognita*) on **tobacco roots**.
- Vector: ***Agrobacterium***.
- Plant makes **sense + anti-sense RNA** of nematode genes → **dsRNA** → silences the nematode's mRNA.
- The nematode cannot survive in the transgenic plant.
:::

:::formula{title="Insulin structure"}
- Two short chains, **A and B**, joined by **disulphide bridges**.
- Made in the body as **pro-insulin**, with an extra **C peptide** that is removed.
- Eli Lilly made chains A and B **separately** in *E. coli*, then joined them.
:::
