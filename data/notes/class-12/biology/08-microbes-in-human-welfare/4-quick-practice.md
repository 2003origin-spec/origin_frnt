## Household and industrial products

:::question{title="1. Holes in cheese" exam="NEET level"}
Which organism, and which gas, cause the large holes in Swiss cheese?

---

The bacterium ***Propionibacterium sharmanii***, which makes a large amount of **CO₂**.
:::

:::question{title="2. Distilled drinks" exam="NEET level"}
Which of these is made **without** distilling the fermented broth: whisky, rum, beer, brandy?

---

**Beer.** Wine and beer are not distilled; whisky, brandy and rum are.
:::

:::question{title="3. Odd one out" exam="NEET level"}
Which pair is wrongly matched?

- (a) Citric acid: *Aspergillus niger*
- (b) Butyric acid: *Acetobacter aceti*
- (c) Lactic acid: *Lactobacillus*
- (d) Ethanol: *Saccharomyces cerevisiae*

---

**(b).** Butyric acid comes from *Clostridium butylicum*. *Acetobacter aceti* makes acetic acid.
:::

:::question{title="4. Match the column" exam="NEET level"}
Match each molecule with its use.

| Molecule | Use |
| --- | --- |
| A. Streptokinase | i. Lowers blood cholesterol |
| B. Cyclosporin A | ii. Clears fruit juice |
| C. Statins | iii. Removes blood clots |
| D. Pectinase | iv. Immunosuppressant |

---

**A–iii, B–iv, C–i, D–ii.**
:::

:::question{title="5. Penicillin facts" exam="NEET level"}
- Statement I: Fleming noticed that *Staphylococci* could not grow around a mould in his culture plate.
- Statement II: Fleming alone showed the full potential of penicillin as a medicine.

Which is correct?

---

**Only Statement I is correct.** Ernest Chain and Howard Florey established its full potential later.
:::

## Sewage and biogas

:::question{title="6. Which tank?" exam="NEET level"}
In a sewage plant, in which unit is BOD reduced the most, and by which kind of microbes?

---

In the **aeration tanks** of secondary treatment, by **aerobic** microbes growing as flocs.
:::

:::question{title="7. BOD readings" exam="NEET level"}
A lake sample has a BOD of 5 mg/L. A sample from an STP inlet has 300 mg/L. Which one has more organic matter, and why?

---

**The STP inlet sample.** BOD rises with the amount of organic matter that microbes can oxidise, so the higher BOD shows more organic matter.
:::

:::question{title="8. Assertion and reason" exam="NEET level"}
- Assertion (A): Cattle dung is used to make biogas.
- Reason (R): The rumen of cattle contains methanogens that break down cellulose.

- (a) Both true, R explains A
- (b) Both true, R does not explain A
- (c) A true, R false
- (d) A false, R true

---

**(a).** Rumen methanogens end up in the dung, so dung is rich in them and makes good gobar gas.
:::

## Biocontrol and biofertilisers

:::question{title="9. Bt action" exam="NEET level"}
*Bacillus thuringiensis* spores are sprayed on a cabbage field. Where does the toxin act, and on which pests?

---

It acts in the **gut of the larvae** after they eat the spores, killing **butterfly caterpillars**. Other insects are left unharmed.
:::

:::question{title="10. Phosphorus supply" exam="NEET level"}
A plant gets most of its extra phosphorus from a root partner. Which of these is the partner: *Rhizobium*, *Glomus*, *Azotobacter*, *Nostoc*?

---

***Glomus***, a mycorrhizal fungus. The other three fix nitrogen.
:::
