## Key terms

:::formula{title="Words to know"}
- **LAB (lactic acid bacteria):** *Lactobacillus* and others that turn milk into curd.
- **Inoculum (starter):** a small amount of curd added to fresh milk. It carries millions of LAB.
- **Fermentor:** a very large vessel for growing microbes on an industrial scale.
- **Antibiotic:** a chemical made by some microbes that kills or slows the growth of other (disease-causing) microbes.
- **Sewage:** municipal waste water. A major component is human excreta.
- **Flocs:** masses of bacteria joined with fungal filaments into mesh-like structures.
- **Activated sludge:** the flocs that settle out after secondary treatment.
- **Methanogens:** bacteria that grow anaerobically on cellulose and make a lot of methane.
- **Biocontrol:** using biological methods to control plant diseases and pests.
- **Biofertilisers:** organisms that enrich the nutrient quality of the soil.
:::

:::formula{title="BOD (biochemical oxygen demand)"}
The amount of oxygen that would be used if **all the organic matter in one litre of water** were oxidised by bacteria.

Higher BOD = more organic matter = **more polluting**.
:::

## Penicillin

:::formula{title="The first antibiotic"}
- Found by chance by **Alexander Fleming**, working on *Staphylococci*.
- Made by the mould ***Penicillium notatum***.
- Full potential shown by **Ernest Chain** and **Howard Florey**.
- Used for American soldiers wounded in World War II.
- **Nobel Prize 1945** to Fleming, Chain and Florey.
:::

## Sewage treatment in two stages

| Stage | Nature | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Physical: filtration, sedimentation | Primary sludge + effluent |
| Secondary | Biological: aerobic microbes in aeration tanks | Activated sludge + treated effluent |

Anaerobic sludge digesters give **biogas**: methane, hydrogen sulphide and CO₂.

## Biogas plant

:::formula{title="Numbers and names"}
- Concrete tank **10–15 feet** deep, fed with dung slurry.
- Floating cover rises as gas forms.
- Main gas: **methane**. Methanogens also make CO₂ and H₂.
- Common methanogen: ***Methanobacterium***.
- Technology developed by **IARI** and **KVIC**.
:::
