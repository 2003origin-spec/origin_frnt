## Diseases

:::question{title="1. Fever every few days" exam="NEET level"}
In malaria, which substance released from burst red blood cells causes the recurring chill and high fever?

---

**Haemozoin.** It is a toxic substance released when the parasites burst the RBCs.
:::

:::question{title="2. Where fertilisation happens" exam="NEET level"}
In the life cycle of *Plasmodium*, where do the gametes fuse?

---

**In the gut of the female *Anopheles* mosquito.** Gametocytes form in human RBCs, but fertilisation and development happen in the mosquito.
:::

:::question{title="3. Match the pathogen" exam="NEET level"}

## Immunity

:::question{title="4. Which barrier?" exam="NEET level"}
Tears and saliva that prevent microbial growth belong to which type of innate barrier?

---

**Physiological barrier.** Skin and mucus are physical barriers; interferons are cytokine barriers.
:::

:::question{title="5. Snakebite" exam="NEET level"}
A snakebite patient is given an injection of antibodies against the venom. What kind of immunity is this, and why is it used?

---

**Passive immunisation.** Ready-made antibodies act at once, while the body's own (active) response would be too slow.
:::

:::question{title="6. Statements on lymphoid organs" exam="NEET level"}
Which statements are correct?
- I. Bone marrow and thymus are primary lymphoid organs.
- II. The thymus grows larger after puberty.
- III. MALT forms about 50% of the lymphoid tissue in the body.
- IV. Peyer's patches are primary lymphoid organs.

---

**Only I and III.** The thymus shrinks with age and is very small by puberty. Peyer's patches are secondary lymphoid organs.
:::

:::question{title="7. Assertion and reason: allergy" exam="NEET level"}
**Assertion (A):** Anti-histamines reduce the symptoms of allergy.
**Reason (R):** In allergy, mast cells release histamine and serotonin, and the antibodies made are of IgE type.

- (a) Both true, R explains A.
- (b) Both true, R does not explain A.
- (c) A true, R false.
- (d) A false, R true.

---

**(a).** Allergy symptoms come from histamine released by mast cells, so blocking histamine relieves them.
:::

## AIDS, cancer and drugs

:::question{title="8. HIV factory" exam="NEET level"}
Which cells keep producing HIV and act as an "HIV factory", and which cells fall steadily in number?

---

**Macrophages** are the HIV factory. The number of **helper T-lymphocytes** falls steadily.
:::

:::question{title="9. Benign or malignant" exam="NEET level"}
A tumor's cells break away, travel through the blood and start new tumors in distant organs. What is this property called, and which tumors show it?

---

**Metastasis**, shown only by **malignant** tumors. Benign tumors stay where they started.
:::

:::question{title="10. Drug and its action" exam="NEET level"}
Which drug, obtained from *Erythroxylum coca*, interferes with dopamine transport and is a strong stimulant of the central nervous system?

---

**Cocaine** (coke or crack). Heroin, by contrast, is a depressant.
:::
