## Health

:::formula{title="What health means"}
Health is a state of **complete physical, mental and social well-being**. It is not just the absence of disease or physical fitness.

Health is affected by: (i) genetic disorders, (ii) infections, (iii) life style (food, water, rest, exercise, habits).
:::

- **Infectious disease:** easily passed from one person to another (AIDS is fatal).
- **Non-infectious disease:** not passed on. **Cancer** is the major cause of death among them.
- **Pathogen:** an organism that causes disease. Most parasites are pathogens.

## Common diseases: bacteria and viruses

| Disease | Pathogen | Spread by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Typhoid | *Salmonella typhi* | Contaminated food and water |
| Pneumonia | *Streptococcus pneumoniae*, *Haemophilus influenzae* | Droplets; shared glasses, utensils |
| Common cold | Rhino viruses | Droplets; contaminated objects |

## Common diseases: protozoans, worms, fungi

| Disease | Pathogen | Spread by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Malaria | *Plasmodium* | Female *Anopheles* mosquito |
| Amoebiasis | *Entamoeba histolytica* | Faecal contamination; houseflies carry it |
| Ascariasis | *Ascaris* (round worm) | Water, vegetables, fruits with eggs |
| Filariasis | *Wuchereria* (filarial worm) | Female mosquito bite |
| Ringworm | *Microsporum*, *Trichophyton*, *Epidermophyton* | Soil; shared towels, clothes, comb |

## Where each pathogen lives or attacks

| Disease | Site |
| --- | --- |
| Typhoid | Enters small intestine, spreads by blood |
| Pneumonia | Alveoli of the lungs |
| Common cold | Nose and respiratory passage, **not** lungs |
| Amoebiasis | Large intestine |
| Ascariasis | Intestine |
| Filariasis | Lymphatic vessels of lower limbs; genital organs |
| Ringworm | Skin, nails, scalp |

## Numbers and tests

:::formula{title="Numbers to know"}
- Typhoid fever: sustained **39° to 40°C**
- Malaria chill and fever: every **3 to 4 days**
- Common cold lasts: **3–7 days**
- AIDS first reported: **1981**; over **25 million** deaths
- Time from HIV infection to AIDS: months to years, **usually 5–10 years**
- MALT: about **50%** of the body's lymphoid tissue
- Adolescence: **12–18 years**
- Tobacco used by humans: over **400 years**
:::

## Immunity at a glance

| Barrier (innate) | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Physical | Skin; mucus of respiratory, gut, urogenital tracts |
| Physiological | Stomach acid, saliva, tears |
| Cellular | PMNL-neutrophils, monocytes, NK cells, macrophages |
| Cytokine | Interferons |

| Acquired response | Done by | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Humoral | B-cells (antibodies in blood) | Antibody mediated |
| Cell-mediated (CMI) | T-cells | Causes graft rejection |

:::formula{title="Antibody"}
4 peptide chains: **2 light + 2 heavy**, written **$\text{H}_2\text{L}_2$**.

Types named in NCERT: IgA, IgM, IgE, IgG.
- **IgA:** in colostrum
- **IgE:** in allergy
:::

## Active and passive immunity

| | Active | Passive |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Antibodies | Made by the host | Given ready-made |
| Speed | Slow | Quick |
| Examples | Infection; vaccination | Colostrum, placenta, antitoxin, anti-venom |

## Drugs of abuse

| Drug group | Source | Acts on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opioids (heroin, smack) | Latex of *Papaver somniferum* (poppy) | Opioid receptors of CNS and gut |
| Cannabinoids (marijuana, hashish, charas, ganja) | Inflorescences of *Cannabis sativa* | Brain receptors; affect cardiovascular system |
| Coca alkaloid (cocaine, coke, crack) | *Erythroxylum coca* (South America) | Dopamine transport |
