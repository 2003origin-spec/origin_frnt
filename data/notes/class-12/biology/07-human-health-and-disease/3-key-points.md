## Diseases and pathogens

:::trap
**Common cold** infects the nose and respiratory passage, **not the lungs**. **Pneumonia** infects the **alveoli** of the lungs.
:::

:::trap
Malaria and filariasis are both spread by female mosquitoes, but NCERT names the genus only for malaria: **female *Anopheles***. Dengue and chikungunya are spread by ***Aedes***.
:::

- **Houseflies** are only **mechanical carriers** of *Entamoeba*. They are not hosts and the parasite does not develop in them.
- Typhoid enters through the **small intestine**. Amoebiasis is in the **large intestine**.
- Lips and nails turning gray to bluish is a sign of **severe pneumonia**.
- **Typhoid Mary** (Mary Mallon) was a **carrier**: she spread typhoid without being ill herself.
- Ringworm is caused by **fungi**, not worms. Heat and moisture help it.

:::tip
Ringworm fungi: *Microsporum*, *Trichophyton* ("tricho" = hair) and *Epidermophyton* ("epidermis" = skin). The names point to the scalp and skin they attack.
:::

## Malaria

:::remember
- Sporozoites = infectious form, injected by mosquito, stored in its **salivary glands**.
- **Haemozoin** from burst RBCs causes the chill and fever every **3–4 days**.
- **Fertilisation** happens in the **mosquito** (gut). Asexual multiplication happens in the **human** (liver, then RBCs).
- Most dangerous: **malignant malaria** by *P. falciparum*.
:::

:::trap
Gametocytes **form in the human** RBCs, but **fertilise in the mosquito**. Don't say the mosquito makes the gametocytes.
:::

## Immunity

:::trap
**Interferons** are a **cytokine barrier** of **innate** immunity. **α-interferon** is also used in cancer as a **biological response modifier**.
:::

- **NK cells** are lymphocytes, but they belong to **innate** (cellular barrier) immunity.
- Saliva and tears are **physiological** barriers. Mucus is a **physical** barrier.
- **T-cells do not secrete antibodies.** They help B-cells and mediate CMI.
- **Graft rejection** is due to **cell-mediated** immunity, not antibodies.
- Secondary response = **anamnestic** response.

:::remember
Antibody types in the exam:
- **IgA:** colostrum
- **IgE:** allergy
- Antibody formula: **H₂L₂** (2 heavy, 2 light chains)
:::

:::trap
**Vaccination is active** immunity (the body makes its own antibodies). **Antitoxin for tetanus** and **anti-venom for snakebite** are **passive** immunisation (ready-made antibodies).
:::

- Colostrum (IgA) and antibodies from the placenta are NCERT's examples of **passive** immunity.
- Hepatitis B vaccine is made in **yeast** by recombinant DNA technology.
- Allergy chemicals: **histamine and serotonin** from **mast cells**.
- Auto-immune example: **rheumatoid arthritis**.

## Lymphoid organs

:::trap
Spleen, lymph nodes, tonsils, Peyer's patches and appendix are **secondary**. Only **bone marrow and thymus** are **primary**.
:::

- Bone marrow makes **all** blood cells, not only lymphocytes.
- The thymus is large at birth and **shrinks** with age, very small by puberty.
- The spleen is a reservoir of **erythrocytes** and filters blood.
- **MALT** is about **50%** of the body's lymphoid tissue.

## AIDS

:::remember
- HIV is a **retrovirus** with an **RNA** genome. **Reverse transcriptase**: RNA → DNA.
- The **macrophage** is the "HIV factory". The **helper T-cell** ($\text{T}_\text{H}$) count falls.
- Test: **ELISA**. Agency in India: **NACO**.
:::

:::trap
HIV does **not** spread by touch, sharing food or physical contact. Only through **body fluids**. AIDS is **acquired**, not congenital, even though mother-to-child spread happens.
:::

- Anti-retroviral drugs **prolong life**; they do not cure.
- AIDS patients die of other infections: *Mycobacterium*, viruses, fungi, *Toxoplasma*.

## Cancer

- Cancer cells have **lost contact inhibition**.
- **Metastasis** is a property of **malignant** tumors only.
- UV is **non-ionising** but still causes cancer. X-rays and gamma rays are **ionising**.
- **CT uses X-rays.** **MRI uses magnetic fields and non-ionising radiation.**
- Proto-oncogenes (c-onc) are in **normal** cells. Viral oncogenes are in oncogenic viruses.

## Drugs and tobacco

:::trap
**Heroin is a depressant**. **Cocaine is a stimulant** (euphoria, energy). Don't swap them.
:::

- Heroin = **diacetylmorphine**, made by **acetylation of morphine**.
- Opioid receptors: **CNS and gastrointestinal tract**. Cannabinoid receptors: mainly **brain**.
- Cannabinoids affect the **cardiovascular** system. Cocaine affects **dopamine** transport.
- Nicotine → adrenal gland → adrenaline and nor-adrenaline → higher BP and heart rate.
- Smoking: more **CO** in blood, less haem-bound oxygen.
- Tobacco **chewing** → cancer of the **oral cavity**.

:::tip
Drug, plant, action in one line each:
- **Heroin:** poppy (*Papaver somniferum*), depressant.
- **Charas, ganja:** *Cannabis sativa*, heart and blood vessels.
- **Cocaine:** coca (*Erythroxylum coca*), stimulant, dopamine.
- Hallucinogenic plants: *Atropa belladona* (NCERT's spelling; usually *belladonna*), *Datura*.
:::

:::trap
**Addiction** is a **psychological** attachment. **Dependence** is shown by the **withdrawal syndrome** when the regular dose is stopped suddenly. Rising **tolerance** of receptors drives higher doses.
:::

- Shared needles spread **AIDS and hepatitis B**.
- Alcohol damages the **liver (cirrhosis)** and nervous system.
- Anabolic steroids in adolescents cause **stunted growth** (early closure of growth centres of long bones).
