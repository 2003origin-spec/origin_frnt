## Origin of life

:::remember
- Universe: about **13.8 billion** years old. Earth: about **4.5 billion** years.
- Life appeared **500 million years after** the earth formed (about 4 billion years ago).
- First non-cellular life: about **3 billion** years ago. First cells: about **2000 mya**.
:::

:::trap
Miller's flask had **$\mathrm{CH_4}$, $\mathrm{H_2}$, $\mathrm{NH_3}$ and water vapour** at **800°C**. There was **no free oxygen**: the early atmosphere was **reducing**. The product NCERT names is **amino acids**, not proteins or cells.
:::

- Pasteur's experiment disproved **spontaneous generation**. It did **not** explain how the first life arose.
- The early earth had **no atmosphere** at first. The ozone layer formed later.
- Oparin was from **Russia**, Haldane from **England**. Miller was **American**.

## Darwin and Lamarck

- Darwin's fitness is **reproductive fitness**: leaving more progeny, not being stronger.
- The two key concepts of Darwin's theory: **branching descent** and **natural selection**.
- Lamarck: use and disuse, **inheritance of acquired characters** (giraffe neck). Rejected today.
- Darwin either ignored Mendel's factors or kept silent about them. The source of variation was later explained by **mutation** and **population genetics**.

:::trap
**Darwin:** small, **directional** variations, gradual change. **de Vries:** large, **random, directionless** mutations, one big step (**saltation**). Do not swap "directional" and "directionless".
:::

## Homology and analogy

:::tip
**H**omologous = **H**ave the same structure (divergent, common ancestor). **A**nalogous = **A**ct the same (convergent).
:::

:::trap
These are **analogous**, not homologous:
- Sweet potato (root) and potato (stem)
- Eye of octopus and of mammals
- Flippers of penguins and dolphins
- Wings of butterfly and bird

These are **homologous**: thorn of *Bougainvillea* and tendril of *Cucurbita*; vertebrate hearts and brains; forelimbs of whale, bat, cheetah and human.
:::

- Embryological evidence (Heckel) was **disproved** by **von Baer**: embryos never pass through the adult stages of other animals.
- Gill slits in vertebrate embryos are **vestigial**. They work only in fish.

## Industrial melanism

- **Before** industry (1850s): more **white** moths, on white lichen. **After** (1920): more **dark** moths, on sooty trunks.
- In rural areas, melanic moths stayed **low**.

:::trap
Moths did **not** turn dark because of soot. Dark moths were **already present**; predators removed more white ones on dark trunks. No variant was completely wiped out.
:::

- Lichens are **indicators** of pollution: they do **not** grow in polluted areas.
- Antibiotic and pesticide resistance = **evolution by anthropogenic action**, seen in months or years.
- Evolution is **stochastic** (chance), not directed.

## Adaptive radiation and convergence

- **Darwin's finches** (Galapagos) and **Australian marsupials** = adaptive radiation.
- Original finch: **seed-eating**. New forms: insectivorous and vegetarian.
- More than one adaptive radiation in one isolated area = **convergent evolution**.
- Placental wolf and **Tasmanian wolf** (a marsupial) look alike = convergent evolution.

## Hardy-Weinberg

:::remember
- $p + q = 1$ and $p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$.
- $p^2$ = AA, $2pq$ = Aa, $q^2$ = aa.
- Equilibrium = allele frequencies constant = **no** evolution. A change = evolution.
:::

:::trap
$q$ is **not** the frequency of aa people. aa is $q^2$. To get $q$, take the **square root** of the aa frequency first.
:::

:::tip
Five factors that upset the balance: **"Many Mice Drift Right Now"** = Migration (gene flow), Mutation, Drift, Recombination, Natural selection.
:::

- **Founder effect** is a kind of **genetic drift** (chance), not natural selection.
- Gene migration changes frequencies in **both** the old and the new population.

## Natural selection types

:::trap
- **Stabilising:** favours the **mean**. The curve gets taller and narrower.
- **Directional:** favours **one side**. The peak shifts.
- **Disruptive:** favours **both ends**. Two peaks form.
:::

## Timeline (use NCERT's numbers exactly)

:::trap
NCERT gives **350 mya** for jawless fish and **320 mya** for sea weeds and a few plants. Other books give different dates. In NEET, use NCERT's numbers.
:::

- The first organisms to invade land were **plants**, not animals.
- Lobefins (**350 mya**) gave rise to the **first amphibians**. Coelacanth: caught in **South Africa, 1938**.
- Reptiles have **thick-shelled eggs** that do not dry in the sun.
- *Ichthyosaurs*: fish-like reptiles, about **200 mya**. Dinosaurs disappeared about **65 mya**.
- *Tyrannosaurus rex*: about **20 feet** tall.
- Coal came from **giant ferns** (pteridophytes).
- First mammals were like **shrews**.

## Human evolution

:::tip
Brain size climbs in order: *Homo habilis* **650–800** → *Homo erectus* **900** → Neanderthal **1400** cc.
:::

:::trap
- *Ramapithecus* was more **man-like**. *Dryopithecus* was more **ape-like**.
- *Homo habilis* probably did **not** eat meat. *Homo erectus* probably **did**.
- Australopithecines hunted with stone weapons but mainly ate **fruit**.
:::

- *Homo erectus* fossils: **Java, 1891**.
- Neanderthals **buried their dead** and used hides. Lived 1,00,000–40,000 years ago in the near east and central Asia.
- *Homo sapiens* arose in **Africa**.
- Modern *Homo sapiens*: ice age, **75,000–10,000** years ago.
- Cave art 18,000 years ago (**Bhimbetka**, Raisen district, MP). Agriculture 10,000 years ago.
- A **baby** chimpanzee skull looks more like an adult **human** skull than an adult chimpanzee skull.
