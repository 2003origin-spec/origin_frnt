## Miller's experiment (1953)

:::formula{title="Chemical evolution in a flask"}
- **Who:** S.L. Miller, an American scientist.
- **Flask held:** $\mathrm{CH_4}$, $\mathrm{H_2}$, $\mathrm{NH_3}$ and water vapour, at **800°C**.
- **Energy:** electric discharge.
- **Result:** **amino acids** formed.

Others later got sugars, nitrogen bases, pigments and fats. Meteorites hold similar compounds.
:::

## Hardy-Weinberg principle

:::formula{title="Allele frequencies"}
$$p + q = 1$$
$p$ = frequency of allele A, $q$ = frequency of allele a.
:::

:::formula{title="Genotype frequencies"}
$$p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1$$
$p^2$ = AA, $2pq$ = Aa, $q^2$ = aa. This is the expansion of $(p+q)^2$.
:::

:::formula{title="Five factors that disturb the equilibrium"}
1. Gene migration (gene flow)
2. Genetic drift
3. Mutation
4. Genetic recombination
5. Natural selection
:::

:::formula{title="Working back from the recessive phenotype"}
Only aa can be counted directly, so start from $q^2$.

If aa $= 0.16$, then $q = 0.4$, $p = 0.6$.
$$2pq = 2 \times 0.6 \times 0.4 = 0.48$$
:::
