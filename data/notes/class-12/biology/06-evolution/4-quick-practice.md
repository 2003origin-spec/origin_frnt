## Origin of life

:::question{title="1. Miller's flask" exam="NEET level"}
Which gas was **not** put into the flask in Miller's 1953 experiment?
(a) $\mathrm{CH_4}$ (b) $\mathrm{NH_3}$ (c) $\mathrm{O_2}$ (d) $\mathrm{H_2}$

---

The flask held $\mathrm{CH_4}$, $\mathrm{H_2}$, $\mathrm{NH_3}$ and water vapour, like the reducing early atmosphere. **(c) $\mathrm{O_2}$**.
:::

## Evidence and adaptive radiation

:::question{title="2. Odd pair out" exam="NEET level"}
Which pair shows **homology**?
(a) Wings of butterfly and bird (b) Sweet potato and potato (c) Forelimbs of bat and cheetah (d) Flippers of penguin and dolphin

---

Bat and cheetah forelimbs have the same bones (humerus to phalanges) but do different jobs. The other three are analogous. **(c)**.
:::

:::question{title="3. Moths in England" exam="NEET level"}
In a polluted industrial area, dark-winged moths became more common than white-winged moths. What is the best reason?

---

Soot darkened the tree trunks and killed the white lichens. White moths stood out and were eaten by predators, so **dark moths, which were already present, survived and left more offspring** (natural selection).
:::

:::question{title="4. Same island, many beaks" exam="NEET level"}
Many kinds of finches with different beaks evolved from a seed-eating ancestor on the Galapagos Islands. What is this called?

---

Different species evolving in one area from one starting point and spreading into new habitats is **adaptive radiation**.
:::

## Hardy-Weinberg

:::question{title="5. Carriers" exam="NEET level"}
In a population in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, 9% of people show a recessive trait (aa). What fraction are heterozygous (Aa)?

---

$q^2 = 0.09$, so $q = 0.3$ and $p = 0.7$.
$$2pq = 2 \times 0.7 \times 0.3 = 0.42$$
**42%** are heterozygous.
:::

:::question{title="6. Is it in equilibrium?" exam="NEET level"}
A population has 360 AA, 480 Aa and 160 aa individuals. Find $p$, and say whether the population is in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium.

---

Total alleles $= 2 \times 1000 = 2000$. A alleles $= 2(360) + 480 = 1200$, so $p = 0.6$, $q = 0.4$.
Expected: $p^2 = 0.36$, $2pq = 0.48$, $q^2 = 0.16$, which is exactly 360 : 480 : 160. **$p = 0.6$; yes, it is in equilibrium.**
:::

:::question{title="7. Assertion and reason" exam="NEET level"}
**Assertion (A):** A small group that moves away and starts a new population may soon differ greatly from the parent population.
**Reason (R):** Natural selection is the only factor that changes allele frequencies.

(a) Both true, R explains A (b) Both true, R does not explain A (c) A true, R false (d) Both false

---

A is true: this is the **founder effect**, a result of genetic drift (chance). R is false: gene flow, drift, mutation and recombination also change allele frequencies. **(c)**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Which selection?" exam="NEET level"}
Over many generations, more individuals of a population come to have a value **other than the mean**, so the peak of the curve shifts to one side. Name this type of natural selection.

---

**Directional selection.** (Stabilising favours the mean; disruptive favours both ends.)
:::

## History of life and man

:::question{title="9. Match the brains" exam="NEET level"}
Match the hominid with its brain capacity.

| Hominid | Brain |
| --- | --- |
| P. *Homo habilis* | i. About 1400 cc |
| Q. *Homo erectus* | ii. 650–800 cc |
| R. Neanderthal man | iii. About 900 cc |

(a) P-ii, Q-iii, R-i (b) P-iii, Q-ii, R-i (c) P-i, Q-iii, R-ii (d) P-ii, Q-i, R-iii

---

*Homo habilis* 650–800 cc, *Homo erectus* about 900 cc, Neanderthal 1400 cc. **(a) P-ii, Q-iii, R-i**.
:::

:::question{title="10. Correct statements" exam="NEET level"}
How many of these statements are correct, according to NCERT?
1. The first organisms to invade land were plants.
2. Dinosaurs suddenly disappeared about 65 mya.
3. *Dryopithecus* was more man-like than *Ramapithecus*.
4. *Homo erectus* fossils were found in Java in 1891.

(a) One (b) Two (c) Three (d) Four

---

Statement 3 is wrong: *Ramapithecus* was more man-like, *Dryopithecus* more ape-like. Statements 1, 2 and 4 are correct. **(c) Three**.
:::
