## Male system

:::trap
**Sertoli cells nourish** the germ cells. **Leydig cells make androgens.** Leydig cells lie **outside** the seminiferous tubules (interstitial spaces), and they are **not** found in the ovary.
:::

:::trap
**LH acts on Leydig cells; FSH acts on Sertoli cells.** Don't swap them. FSH helps **spermiogenesis** through factors from the Sertoli cells.
:::

- The scrotum keeps the testes **2–2.5 °C cooler** than the body. Spermatogenesis needs this.
- The **epididymis** lies along the **posterior** surface of each testis.
- The **vas deferens** loops over the **urinary bladder** and, with the duct from the seminal vesicle, forms the **ejaculatory duct**.
- The **prostate is single**; seminal vesicles and bulbourethral glands are **paired**.
- Seminal plasma is rich in **fructose**, calcium and enzymes (fructose is the sugar, not glucose).

:::tip
Order of male ducts (beyond NCERT, a memory aid): "**R**eal **V**ery **E**ager **V**ans **E**xit **U**p" = **R**ete testis, **V**asa efferentia, **E**pididymis, **V**as deferens, **E**jaculatory duct, **U**rethra.
:::

## Female system

- Oviduct order from the ovary: **infundibulum → ampulla → isthmus** → uterus. **Fimbriae** belong to the infundibulum.
- Uterine layers outside in: **perimetrium, myometrium, endometrium**. The **endometrium** changes each cycle; the **myometrium** contracts at delivery.
- **Birth canal** = cervical canal + vagina.
- The **clitoris** lies above the **urethral opening**, at the upper junction of the labia minora.

:::remember
The presence or absence of the **hymen** is **not a reliable indicator** of virginity or sexual experience.
:::

## Spermatogenesis and oogenesis

:::trap
**Spermiogenesis** = spermatids change into sperms. **Spermiation** = sperms are released from the seminiferous tubules. **Spermatogenesis** = the whole process. Three different words.
:::

:::trap
What is released at **ovulation** is the **secondary oocyte**. NCERT calls it the "ovum", but it has not finished meiosis. Meiosis II is completed only **after a sperm enters** it, giving the second polar body and the ootid.
:::

- The **primary oocyte** stays arrested in **prophase I** from before birth until the **tertiary follicle** stage. It completes meiosis I there.
- The **antrum** first appears in the **tertiary** follicle. The theca first appears in the **secondary** follicle.
- The **zona pellucida** is formed by the secondary oocyte in the **Graafian follicle**.
- No new oogonia are formed **after birth**. Sperm formation continues even in old men.
- Both meiotic divisions in the female are **unequal**; in the male both are **equal**.
- Spermatids and secondary spermatocytes both have **23** chromosomes. Primary spermatocytes have **46**.

## Menstrual cycle

:::remember
- Ovulation (about **day 14**) is caused by the **LH surge**.
- The **corpus luteum** secretes **progesterone**, which maintains the endometrium.
- No fertilisation → corpus luteum degenerates → menstruation.
:::

:::trap
The **corpus luteum** is not a site of oogenesis. It forms from the **remaining parts of the Graafian follicle** after ovulation.
:::

- The menstrual cycle is found only in **female primates** (monkeys, apes, humans).
- Menstrual cycles **stop during pregnancy**.
- Missed periods may mean pregnancy, but stress or poor health can also cause them.
- **Menarche** = first menstruation. **Menopause** = cycles stop, around 50.

## Fertilisation and implantation

- Fertilisation happens in the **ampullary region** of the fallopian tube, not in the uterus.
- Cleavage starts as the zygote moves through the **isthmus**.
- **Morula** = 8 to 16 blastomeres. **Blastocyst** = trophoblast + inner cell mass.
- The **trophoblast** attaches to the endometrium. The **inner cell mass** becomes the embryo.

:::remember
The **father** decides the sex of the baby: every ovum carries X, but a sperm carries X or Y (50% each). So blaming the mother for a daughter is scientifically wrong.
:::

:::tip
One ovum per cycle. **Identical** twins come from **one** ovum (one zygote that splits). **Fraternal** twins come from **two** ova.
:::

## Placenta and hormones

:::trap
**Relaxin** is made by the **ovary**, not the placenta, in the later phase of pregnancy. Yet hCG, hPL and relaxin are all made **only during pregnancy**.
:::

- The placenta is formed by **chorionic villi + uterine tissue**.
- Placental hormones: **hCG, hPL, estrogens, progestogens**.
- Raised in the mother's blood: estrogens, progestogens, **cortisol, prolactin, thyroxine**.
- Germ layers: ectoderm and endoderm first; **mesoderm** appears later, between them.
- The inner cell mass contains **stem cells**, which can form all tissues and organs.

## Development, parturition, lactation

:::remember
- **1 month:** heart. **2nd month:** limbs and digits.
- **12 weeks:** most organ systems. **5th month:** first movements, hair on head.
- **24 weeks:** fine body hair, eyelids separate, eyelashes.
:::

- The first signal for parturition comes from the **fully developed foetus and the placenta** (foetal ejection reflex), not from the mother.
- **Oxytocin** (from the maternal pituitary) and uterine contraction push each other up. NCERT calls this a **stimulatory reflex** (a positive feedback loop). NCERT asks what doctors inject to induce delivery: the answer is oxytocin.
- The **placenta** is expelled **after** the baby.

:::trap
Hormones of parturition: the chapter text describes only **oxytocin**, but NCERT's summary names **cortisol, estrogens and oxytocin**. Know all three.
:::

- **Colostrum** (first few days of milk) is rich in **antibodies**.
