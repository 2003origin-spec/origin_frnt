## The reproductive events, in order

:::formula{title="From gametes to birth"}
1. **Gametogenesis:** forming sperms and ova.
2. **Insemination:** sperms placed in the female genital tract.
3. **Fertilisation:** sperm and ovum fuse to form a zygote.
4. **Implantation:** the blastocyst attaches to the uterine wall.
5. **Gestation:** embryonic development.
6. **Parturition:** delivery of the baby.

Humans reproduce **sexually**, are **viviparous**, and fertilisation is **internal**.
:::

## Cells and their jobs

| Cell or part | Where | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spermatogonia | Inside wall of seminiferous tubule | Form sperms |
| Sertoli cells | Seminiferous tubule | Nourish germ cells |
| Leydig cells | Interstitial spaces | Secrete androgens |
| Granulosa cells | Around the oocyte | Form follicle layers |
| Corpus luteum | Ovary, after ovulation | Secretes progesterone |
| Trophoblast | Outer layer of blastocyst | Attaches to endometrium |
| Inner cell mass | Inside blastocyst | Becomes the embryo |

## Duct and gland pathways

:::formula{title="Path of sperms"}
Seminiferous tubules → rete testis → vasa efferentia → epididymis → vas deferens → ejaculatory duct → urethra → urethral meatus
:::

:::formula{title="Male accessory glands"}
- Seminal vesicles (**paired**)
- Prostate (**single**)
- Bulbourethral glands (**paired**): also lubricate the penis

Their secretions form **seminal plasma**: rich in **fructose, calcium and certain enzymes**.

Semen = seminal plasma + sperms.
:::

:::formula{title="Parts of the oviduct (ovary to uterus)"}
Infundibulum (with fimbriae) → ampulla (wider) → isthmus (narrow lumen) → uterus
:::

:::formula{title="Path of milk"}
Alveoli → mammary tubules → mammary duct → mammary ampulla → lactiferous duct → nipple
:::

## Gametogenesis in numbers

| Stage | Chromosomes |
| --- | --- |
| Spermatogonium / oogonium | 46 (2n) |
| Primary spermatocyte / oocyte | 46 (2n) |
| Secondary spermatocyte / oocyte | 23 (n) |
| Spermatid / ovum (ootid) | 23 (n) |
| Zygote | 46 (2n) |

:::formula{title="Output of one cell"}
- 1 primary spermatocyte → **4** sperms (equal divisions).
- 1 primary oocyte → **1** ovum + polar bodies (unequal divisions).
:::

## Hormones

| Hormone | Source | Main action here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GnRH | Hypothalamus | Releases LH and FSH |
| LH | Anterior pituitary | Leydig cells; LH surge → ovulation |
| FSH | Anterior pituitary | Sertoli cells; follicle growth |
| Androgens | Leydig cells | Spermatogenesis; accessory ducts |
| Estrogens | Growing follicles | Rise in follicular phase |
| Progesterone | Corpus luteum; placenta | Maintains endometrium |
| hCG, hPL | Placenta | Only in pregnancy |
| Relaxin | Ovary (late pregnancy) | Only in pregnancy |
| Oxytocin | Maternal pituitary | Uterine contractions |
