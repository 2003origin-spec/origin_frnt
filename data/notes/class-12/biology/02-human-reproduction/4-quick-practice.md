## Reproductive systems

:::question{title="1. Androgen source" exam="NEET level"}
A drug destroys only the cells in the interstitial spaces of the testis. Which hormone secretion falls?

---

The interstitial spaces hold the **Leydig cells**, so **androgen** secretion falls.
:::

:::question{title="2. Oviduct order" exam="NEET level"}
An ovum travels from the ovary towards the uterus. In which order does it pass the parts of the oviduct?

---

**Infundibulum → ampulla → isthmus.** The fimbriae of the infundibulum collect it first.
:::

:::question{title="3. Match the column" exam="NEET level"}
Match:
- (a) Myometrium (b) Endometrium (c) Acrosome (d) Middle piece
- (i) Enzymes for entering the ovum (ii) Energy for tail movement (iii) Strong contractions at delivery (iv) Cyclical changes each month

---

**a–iii, b–iv, c–i, d–ii.**
:::

## Gametogenesis

:::question{title="4. Counting sperms" exam="NEET level"}
How many sperms are formed from 25 primary spermatocytes, and how many chromosomes does each sperm carry?

---

Each primary spermatocyte gives 4 spermatids. So $25 \times 4 =$ **100 sperms, each with 23 chromosomes**.
:::

:::question{title="5. Oocyte stages" exam="NEET level"}
In which follicle does the primary oocyte complete its first meiotic division?

---

In the **tertiary follicle** (the one with the antrum). It forms a secondary oocyte and the first polar body.
:::

:::question{title="6. Statements on oogenesis" exam="NEET level"}
Which statement is correct?
- (A) New oogonia keep forming after puberty.
- (B) The ovum released at ovulation has finished meiosis II.
- (C) Meiosis II of the secondary oocyte is completed after sperm entry.
- (D) Both meiotic divisions in the female are equal.

---

**(C).** Oogonia form only before birth, the released cell is a secondary oocyte, and both divisions are unequal.
:::

## Cycle, fertilisation, pregnancy

:::question{title="7. LH surge" exam="NEET level"}
A woman has a 28-day cycle. Around which day does the LH surge occur, and what does it cause?

---

Around **day 14**. It causes the Graafian follicle to rupture and release the ovum: **ovulation**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Assertion and reason" exam="NEET level"}
**Assertion:** Not every copulation leads to pregnancy.
**Reason:** Fertilisation occurs only if the ovum and sperms reach the ampullary region at the same time.

(A) Both true, R explains A. (B) Both true, R does not explain A. (C) A true, R false. (D) Both false.

---

**(A).** NCERT gives exactly this reason.
:::

:::question{title="9. Pregnancy hormones" exam="NEET level"}
Which hormone of pregnancy is secreted by the ovary and not by the placenta?

---

**Relaxin**, in the later phase of pregnancy.
:::

:::question{title="10. Parturition signal" exam="NEET level"}
Where do the signals that start parturition come from, and which hormone then strengthens the contractions?

---

From the **fully developed foetus and the placenta** (foetal ejection reflex). **Oxytocin** from the maternal pituitary then strengthens the contractions.
:::
