## Male and female gametophytes

:::question{title="1. Wall layers" exam="NEET level"}
Which wall layer of the microsporangium nourishes the developing pollen grains?

(a) Endothecium (b) Middle layers (c) Tapetum (d) Epidermis

---

The innermost layer, the **tapetum (c)**, nourishes the pollen. The outer three layers protect and help dehiscence.
:::

:::question{title="2. Counting pollen" exam="NEET level"}
An anther has 40 pollen mother cells, and every one completes meiosis. How many pollen grains form, and how many male gametes can they give in all?

---

Each PMC gives 4 microspores: $40 \times 4 = 160$ pollen grains. Each pollen grain gives 2 male gametes: **160 pollen grains and 320 male gametes**.
:::

:::question{title="3. Ploidy" exam="NEET level"}
In an angiosperm with $2n = 24$, how many chromosomes are there in a synergid, a nucellus cell and an endosperm cell?

---

Synergid is n, nucellus is 2n, endosperm is 3n: **12, 24 and 36**.
:::

:::question{title="4. Embryo sac" exam="NEET level"}
How many mitotic nuclear divisions turn a functional megaspore into an 8-nucleate embryo sac, and how many cells does the mature embryo sac have?

---

**3 free-nuclear mitoses** (1 → 2 → 4 → 8 nuclei), and the mature embryo sac has **7 cells**, because the 2 polar nuclei share one central cell.
:::

## Pollination

:::question{title="5. Same plant" exam="NEET level"}
A bee carries pollen from one flower of a mango tree to another flower on the same tree. Name the type of pollination. Is it genetically self or cross?

---

**Geitonogamy.** It needs an agent, but the pollen comes from the same plant, so genetically it is **self-pollination**.
:::

:::question{title="6. Outbreeding devices" exam="NEET level"}
Which condition prevents both autogamy and geitonogamy?

(a) Monoecy, as in maize (b) Dioecy, as in papaya (c) Cleistogamy (d) Anther and stigma at different positions

---

**(b) Dioecy.** Each plant is male or female, so no pollen from the same plant can reach the stigma. Monoecy and different positions stop only autogamy; cleistogamy forces autogamy.
:::

## Fertilisation and after

:::question{title="8. Assertion and reason" exam="NEET level"}
**Assertion:** Fertilisation in angiosperms is called double fertilisation.

**Reason:** Two male gametes fuse with the egg cell.

(a) Both true, and R explains A (b) Both true, but R does not explain A (c) A true, R false (d) Both false

---

**(c).** Double fertilisation means two fusions in one embryo sac: syngamy (one male gamete + egg) and triple fusion (the other male gamete + two polar nuclei). Only one male gamete fuses with the egg.
:::

:::question{title="9. Statements" exam="NEET level"}
How many of these statements are correct?
1. Perisperm is the remnant of the nucellus.
2. Castor seed is non-albuminous.
3. In apple, the thalamus also forms part of the fruit.
4. Coconut water is cellular endosperm.

---

1 is correct (black pepper, beet). 2 is wrong: castor is albuminous. 3 is correct (a false fruit). 4 is wrong: coconut water is free-nuclear endosperm. **Two** are correct.
:::

:::question{title="10. Orange seed" exam="NEET level"}
An orange seed has several embryos. Most of them grew from nucellar cells. What are these phenomena called, and how do these embryos compare with the mother plant?

---

**Polyembryony**, here caused by **apomixis** (nucellar embryos). They come from the mother's own diploid cells without fertilisation, so they are **genetically identical to the mother**.
:::
