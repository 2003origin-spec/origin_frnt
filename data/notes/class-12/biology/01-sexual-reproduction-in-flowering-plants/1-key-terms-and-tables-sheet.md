## Male side: anther to pollen

:::formula{title="The anther"}
Bilobed, **dithecous** (2 theca per lobe), **tetrasporangiate** (4 microsporangia, 2 per lobe, at the corners).

Microsporangia become **pollen sacs**.
:::

:::formula{title="Wall layers of a microsporangium (outside to inside)"}
1. Epidermis
2. Endothecium
3. Middle layers
4. **Tapetum** (innermost)

Outer three: protection and dehiscence. Tapetum: **nourishes** the pollen. Its cells have dense cytoplasm and often more than one nucleus.
:::

:::formula{title="Sequence (microsporogenesis and after)"}
Sporogenous tissue → PMC (2n) → (meiosis) → microspore tetrad (n) → pollen grain (vegetative + generative cell)

Generative cell → (mitosis) → 2 male gametes
:::

:::formula{title="Pollen grain"}
- Size: about **25–50 µm**, generally spherical.
- **Exine:** hard, of **sporopollenin**. **Germ pores** are apertures where sporopollenin is absent.
- **Intine:** thin, continuous, of **cellulose and pectin**.
- Mature: **vegetative cell** (big, food, irregular nucleus) + **generative cell** (small, spindle-shaped).
- Over **60%** of angiosperms shed pollen at the **2-celled** stage; the rest at the 3-celled stage.
:::

## Female side: pistil to embryo sac

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Monocarpellary | One pistil |
| Multicarpellary | More than one pistil |
| Syncarpous | Pistils fused (*Papaver*) |
| Apocarpous | Pistils free (*Michelia*) |

:::formula{title="Parts of the ovule"}
- **Funicle:** stalk joining ovule to placenta.
- **Hilum:** where the ovule body meets the funicle.
- **Integuments:** one or two envelopes.
- **Micropyle:** opening at the tip.
- **Chalaza:** basal part, opposite the micropyle.
- **Nucellus:** food-rich mass of cells; holds the embryo sac.
:::

:::formula{title="Embryo sac (monosporic, 8-nucleate, 7-celled)"}
1 functional megaspore → 3 free-nuclear mitoses → 8 nuclei.

- Micropylar end: **egg apparatus** = 2 synergids + 1 egg
- Chalazal end: **3 antipodals**
- Centre: **central cell** with **2 polar nuclei**

$$3 + 3 + 1 = 7 \text{ cells}$$
$$3 + 3 + 2 = 8 \text{ nuclei}$$
:::

## Outbreeding devices

| Device | Blocks same-flower pollen | Blocks same-plant pollen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pollen and stigma not synchronised | Yes | No |
| Anther and stigma apart | Yes | No |
| Self-incompatibility | Yes | Yes |
| Monoecy (castor, maize) | Yes | No |
| Dioecy (papaya) | Yes | Yes |

Self-incompatibility lets the self-pollen land, but stops it from germinating or growing a tube, so it can't fertilise.

## Double fertilisation

:::formula{title="Two fusions in one embryo sac"}
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Syngamy: } & \text{male gamete (n)} \\ & + \text{egg (n)} \\ & \to \text{zygote (2n)} \end{aligned}$$
$$\begin{aligned} \text{Triple fusion: } & \text{male gamete (n)} \\ & + 2 \text{ polar nuclei (n + n)} \\ & \to \text{PEN (3n)} \end{aligned}$$
Zygote → embryo. Primary endosperm cell → endosperm.
:::
