## Crosses and ratios

:::question{title="1. Pure tall plants" exam="NEET level"}
In pea, tall (T) is dominant over dwarf (t). A Tt plant is selfed. What fraction of the **tall** offspring are true-breeding?

---

The offspring are 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt. Of the 3 tall ones, only TT is true-breeding: **1/3**.
:::

:::question{title="2. Test cross" exam="NEET level"}
A tall pea plant is crossed with a dwarf plant, and half the offspring are dwarf. What was the genotype of the tall parent?

---

**Tt.** Tt × tt gives 1 Tt (tall) : 1 tt (dwarf). A TT parent would have given only tall plants.
:::

:::question{title="3. Snapdragon F2" exam="NEET level"}
Pink snapdragons (Rr) are crossed with each other and give 400 plants. How many of each colour would you expect?

---

The ratio is 1 RR : 2 Rr : 1 rr, so about **100 red, 200 pink and 100 white**.
:::

:::question{title="4. Blood groups" exam="NEET level"}
A man of blood group A ($I^Ai$) marries a woman of blood group B ($I^Bi$). Which blood groups can their children have?

---

The children can be $I^AI^B$, $I^Ai$, $I^Bi$ or $ii$, each with a chance of 1/4: groups **AB, A, B and O**, all four.
:::

:::question{title="5. Kinds of gametes" exam="NEET level"}
How many kinds of gametes does a plant of genotype AaBbCCDd make?

---

There are 3 heterozygous pairs (Aa, Bb, Dd); CC is homozygous and adds nothing. So $2^3 =$ **8** kinds.
:::

:::question{title="6. Dihybrid F2" exam="NEET level"}
RrYy pea plants (round yellow) are selfed and give 1600 offspring. About how many are round and green?

---

Round green is 3 of the 16 parts in 9 : 3 : 3 : 1. $\dfrac{3}{16} \times 1600 =$ **300**.
:::

## Linkage, sex and disorders

:::question{title="7. Map distance" exam="NEET level"}
In a test cross, 1000 offspring are counted: 450 and 450 look like the two parents, and 50 and 50 show new combinations. How far apart are the two genes?

---

Recombination frequency $= \dfrac{50 + 50}{1000} \times 100 = 10\%$, so the genes are **10 map units** (10 cM) apart.
:::

:::question{title="8. Birds" exam="NEET level"}
In birds, which parent decides the sex of the chick?

---

**The mother.** Females are ZW and males ZZ, so the egg carries either Z or W, while every sperm carries Z.
:::

:::question{title="9. Colour blindness" exam="NEET level"}
A colour-blind man marries a woman with normal vision who is not a carrier. What is the chance that their son is colour-blind?

---

**Zero.** A son gets his X from his mother, and hers are normal. All their daughters will be carriers, because each gets the father's X.
:::

:::question{title="10. Karyotype" exam="NEET level"}
A person's karyotype shows 47 chromosomes, with the sex chromosomes XXY. Name the condition and the sex of the person.

---

**Klinefelter syndrome.** The person is male (with some female features, such as breast development) and sterile.
:::
