## Restriction enzymes

:::question{title="1. Reading the name" exam="NEET level"}
In the name of the enzyme EcoRI, what does the letter **R** stand for?

---

**The strain of the bacterium (RY 13).** E is the genus *Escherichia*, co the species *coli*, and I the order of isolation.
:::

:::question{title="2. Is it a palindrome?" exam="NEET level"}
Which of these, written 5′ → 3′ on one strand, is a palindrome in the DNA sense?

(a) GATTAG (b) GGATCC (c) GAATTG (d) AAGCTA

---

Write the complementary strand and read it 5′ → 3′. Only GGATCC gives GGATCC back. **(b)**
:::

:::question{title="3. Sticky ends" exam="NEET level"}
EcoRI cuts 5′-GAATTC-3′ between G and A on both strands. Which bases form the single-stranded overhang at each cut end?

---

The cut leaves G on one side and AATTC on the other, on both strands. The overhang is **AATT**.
:::

## Separating DNA

:::question{title="4. Gel electrophoresis" exam="NEET level"}
DNA fragments of 500 bp, 2000 bp and 5000 bp are run on an agarose gel. Which band ends up farthest from the wells, and which electrode does it move towards?

---

**The 500 bp band, moving towards the anode.** DNA is negatively charged, and the smallest fragment moves fastest through the agarose sieve.
:::

:::question{title="5. Seeing the bands" exam="NEET level"}
Which treatment lets you see DNA bands in an agarose gel?

---

**Staining with ethidium bromide, then exposure to UV light.** The bands glow bright orange.
:::

## Vectors and selection

:::question{title="6. pBR322 plates" exam="NEET level"}
A foreign gene is inserted at the BamH I site of pBR322 and the plasmids are put into *E. coli*. A colony grows on ampicillin but not on tetracycline. What does it carry?

---

**A recombinant plasmid.** The insert inactivated $\text{tet}^R$, so the cell lost tetracycline resistance but kept ampicillin resistance. A non-recombinant would grow on both.
:::

:::question{title="7. Blue and colourless" exam="NEET level"}
**Assertion (A):** In blue-white selection, colourless colonies carry recombinant DNA.

**Reason (R):** An insert in the β-galactosidase gene stops the enzyme being made, so the chromogenic substrate is not turned blue.

(a) Both true, R explains A (b) Both true, R does not explain A (c) A true, R false (d) A false, R true

---

The insert causes insertional inactivation, so no blue colour forms. **(a)**
:::

:::question{title="8. Match the method" exam="NEET level"}
Match each method to its use.
- P. Micro-injection
- Q. Biolistics

- S. Disarmed Ti plasmid

1. Bacteria made competent with calcium
2. Plant cells, via *Agrobacterium*
3. Nucleus of an animal cell

**P-3, Q-4, R-1, S-2.**
:::

## Processes

:::question{title="9. Statements on PCR" exam="NEET level"}
Which statements are correct?
- I. Each cycle runs denaturation, primer annealing and extension, in that order.
- II. Taq polymerase is used because it stays active at high temperature.
- III. One primer is enough to copy the region.
- IV. PCR is done in vitro.

---

PCR needs two sets of primers, so III is wrong. **I, II and IV.**
:::

:::question{title="10. Isolating DNA" exam="NEET level"}
To get pure DNA from a fungus, a student uses an enzyme to break the cell wall, removes RNA and protein, and adds one reagent so the DNA comes out as threads. Name the wall enzyme and the reagent.

---

**Chitinase** for the fungal wall, and **chilled ethanol** to precipitate the DNA (ribonuclease and protease remove RNA and protein).
:::
