## Work

:::question{title="1. Work from vectors" exam="NEET level"}
A constant force $\vec F = (3\hat i + 4\hat j)$ N moves a body through a displacement $\vec d = (2\hat i + 5\hat j)$ m. Find the work done by the force.

---

$W = \vec F\cdot\vec d = 3 \times 2 + 4 \times 5 = 6 + 20 =$ **26 J**.
:::

:::question{title="2. Area under F–x" exam="NEET level"}
A force along $+x$ acts on a body. It rises evenly from 0 at $x = 0$ to 10 N at $x = 4$ m, stays at 10 N up to $x = 6$ m, then falls evenly to 0 at $x = 8$ m. Find the work done from $x = 0$ to $x = 8$ m.

---

Work = area under the graph: a triangle, a rectangle and a triangle.

$\tfrac12 \times 4 \times 10 + 2 \times 10 + \tfrac12 \times 2 \times 10 = 20 + 20 + 10 =$ **50 J**.
:::

## Work-energy theorem and energy conservation

:::question{title="3. Braking car" exam="NEET level"}
A 1000 kg car moving at 20 m/s is stopped by a constant braking force of 5000 N. How far does it go before stopping?

---

Work-energy theorem: $F s = \tfrac12 mv^2 = \tfrac12 \times 1000 \times 400 = 2\times10^5$ J.

$s = \dfrac{2\times10^5}{5000} =$ **40 m**.
:::

:::question{title="4. Air resistance" exam="NEET level"}
A 0.5 kg ball is dropped from rest from a height of 20 m. It reaches the ground at 18 m/s. Find the work done by air resistance.

---

Work by gravity $= mgh = 0.5 \times 10 \times 20 = 100$ J. Final KE $= \tfrac12 \times 0.5 \times 18^2 = 81$ J.

$W_g + W_{air} = K_f - 0$, so $W_{air} = 81 - 100 =$ **−19 J**.
:::

:::question{title="5. Spring compression" exam="NEET level"}
A 2 kg block slides at 3 m/s on a smooth floor and hits a spring of spring constant 200 N/m. Find the maximum compression of the spring.

---

All the KE becomes spring PE: $\tfrac12 kx_m^2 = \tfrac12 mv^2$.

$x_m = v\sqrt{\dfrac{m}{k}} = 3 \times \sqrt{\dfrac{2}{200}} = 3 \times 0.1 =$ **0.3 m**.
:::

:::question{title="6. Pendulum released" exam="JEE Main level"}
A 0.2 kg bob hangs on a light string of length 0.8 m. It is pulled aside until the string is horizontal and then released from rest. Find its speed and the tension in the string at the lowest point.

---

Energy conservation: $v = \sqrt{2gL} = \sqrt{2 \times 10 \times 0.8} =$ **4 m/s**.

At the lowest point: $T - mg = \dfrac{mv^2}{L} = \dfrac{0.2 \times 16}{0.8} = 4$ N.

$T = 4 + 0.2 \times 10 =$ **6 N** (three times the weight).
:::

## Power

:::question{title="7. Car at steady speed" exam="NEET level"}
A car moves at a steady 72 km/h on a level road. The total resistive force on it is 600 N. What power does the engine deliver?

---

$72$ km/h $= 20$ m/s. At constant speed the driving force equals the resistance, 600 N.

$P = Fv = 600 \times 20 = 12000$ W $=$ **12 kW**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Constant power" exam="JEE Main level"}
A machine delivers a constant power of 4 W to a 2 kg body that starts from rest on a smooth level surface. Find its speed after 9 s.

---

All the work goes into KE: $Pt = \tfrac12 mv^2$.

$4 \times 9 = \tfrac12 \times 2 \times v^2$, so $v^2 = 36$ and $v =$ **6 m/s**.
:::

## Collisions

:::question{title="9. Elastic collision" exam="NEET level"}
A 2 kg ball moving at 3 m/s makes a head-on elastic collision with a 1 kg ball at rest. Find both velocities after the collision.

---

$v_{1f} = \dfrac{m_1 - m_2}{m_1 + m_2}v_{1i} = \dfrac{1}{3} \times 3 =$ **1 m/s**.

$v_{2f} = \dfrac{2m_1}{m_1 + m_2}v_{1i} = \dfrac{4}{3} \times 3 =$ **4 m/s**.

Both move forward. Check: KE before $= 9$ J, after $= 1 + 8 = 9$ J.
:::

:::question{title="10. Bullet in a block" exam="JEE Main level"}
A 10 g bullet moving at 400 m/s hits a 1.99 kg wooden block hanging at rest on a long string, and stays inside it.

(a) Find the common speed just after the impact and the KE lost.

(b) How high does the block rise?

---

(a) Momentum: $0.01 \times 400 = 2 \times v$, so $v =$ **2 m/s**.

KE before $= \tfrac12 \times 0.01 \times 400^2 = 800$ J. KE after $= \tfrac12 \times 2 \times 2^2 = 4$ J. Lost: **796 J**.

(b) After the impact, energy is conserved: $h = \dfrac{v^2}{2g} = \dfrac{4}{20} =$ **0.2 m**.
:::
