## Momentum and impulse

:::question{title="1. Ball off a wall" exam="NEET level"}
A 0.2 kg ball hits a wall at 20 m/s along the normal and bounces straight back at 15 m/s. The contact lasts 0.05 s. Find the impulse on the ball and the average force on it.

---

Take the direction away from the wall as positive. $\Delta p = 0.2 \times 15 - 0.2 \times (-20) = 7$ N s.

Impulse = **7 N s**, away from the wall. Average force $= \dfrac{7}{0.05} =$ **140 N**.
:::

:::question{title="2. Recoil of a gun" exam="NEET level"}
A 4 kg gun fires a 20 g bullet at 400 m/s. Find the recoil speed of the gun.

---

The total momentum is zero before and after. $MV = mv$, so $V = \dfrac{0.02 \times 400}{4} =$ **2 m/s**, opposite to the bullet.
:::

:::question{title="3. Force from momentum" exam="JEE Main level"}
The momentum of a particle is $\vec p = 2t^2\,\hat i + 3t\,\hat j$ (in kg m/s, $t$ in s). Find the size of the net force on it at $t = 1$ s.

---

$\vec F = \dfrac{d\vec p}{dt} = 4t\,\hat i + 3\,\hat j$. At $t = 1$ s, $\vec F = 4\hat i + 3\hat j$, so $F = \sqrt{16 + 9} =$ **5 N**.
:::

## Lifts and connected bodies

:::question{title="4. Weighing in a lift" exam="NEET level"}
A 60 kg man stands on a weighing scale in a lift. What does the scale read (in N) when the lift accelerates upward at 2 m/s²? What does it read if the cable snaps?

---

$R - mg = ma$, so $R = 60 \times (10 + 2) =$ **720 N**. If the cable snaps, the man and lift fall freely and the reading is **0**.
:::

:::question{title="5. Two blocks in contact" exam="NEET level"}
Blocks of 2 kg and 3 kg touch each other on a smooth floor. A horizontal force of 20 N pushes the 2 kg block. Find the force between the blocks.

---

$a = \dfrac{20}{2 + 3} = 4$ m/s². The 3 kg block is moved only by the contact force: $N = 3 \times 4 =$ **12 N**.
:::

:::question{title="6. Atwood machine" exam="JEE Main level"}
Masses of 3 kg and 2 kg hang from the two ends of a light string over a light, frictionless pulley. Find the acceleration and the tension.

---

$a = \dfrac{(3 - 2) \times 10}{3 + 2} =$ **2 m/s²**.

For the 3 kg mass: $30 - T = 3 \times 2$, so $T =$ **24 N**. (Check: $\dfrac{2 \times 3 \times 2 \times 10}{5} = 24$ N.)
:::

## Friction

:::question{title="7. Does it move?" exam="NEET level"}
A 5 kg block rests on a floor with $\mu_s = 0.4$ and $\mu_k = 0.3$. A horizontal force of 15 N is applied. Find the friction force.

---

Maximum static friction $= 0.4 \times 50 = 20$ N. Since 15 N < 20 N, the block does not move, and friction just balances the push: **15 N**. (Not 20 N, and not $0.3 \times 50$.)
:::

:::question{title="8. Sliding down an incline" exam="NEET level"}
A block slides down a rough incline at $37^\circ$ with $\mu_k = 0.5$. Find its acceleration. Use $\sin 37^\circ = 0.6$, $\cos 37^\circ = 0.8$.

---

$a = g(\sin\theta - \mu_k\cos\theta) = 10(0.6 - 0.5 \times 0.8) =$ **2 m/s²**, down the incline.
:::

:::question{title="9. Block held against a wall" exam="JEE Main level"}
A 2 kg block is pressed against a vertical wall by a horizontal force $F$. $\mu_s$ between the block and the wall is 0.5. Find the least $F$ that stops the block from slipping down.

---

The wall's normal force is $N = F$. Friction (upward) must hold the weight: $\mu_s F \ge mg$. So $F \ge \dfrac{20}{0.5}$, and the least force is **40 N**.
:::

## Circular motion

:::question{title="10. Banked curve" exam="JEE Main level"}
A curve of radius 50 m is banked at $\theta$ with $\tan\theta = 0.75$. The coefficient of static friction is 0.5. Find the greatest speed at which a car can take the curve without skidding.

---

$v_{max}^2 = \dfrac{rg(\mu_s + \tan\theta)}{1 - \mu_s\tan\theta} = \dfrac{500 \times 1.25}{1 - 0.375} = \dfrac{625}{0.625} = 1000$.

$v_{max} = \sqrt{1000} = 10\sqrt{10} \approx$ **31.6 m/s** (about 114 km/h).
:::
