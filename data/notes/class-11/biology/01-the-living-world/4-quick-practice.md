## Naming organisms

:::question{title="1. Correct name" exam="NEET level"}
Which one is written correctly in print?
(a) *Panthera Tigris* (b) *panthera tigris* (c) *Panthera tigris* (d) *Tigris panthera*

---

**(c) *Panthera tigris*.** The genus comes first with a capital letter; the specific epithet comes second with a small letter; both are in italics.
:::

:::question{title="2. Author citation" exam="NEET level"}
In the name *Mangifera indica* Linn., what does "Linn." tell us?

---

It is the author's name in short form. It tells us the species was **first described by Linnaeus**.
:::

:::question{title="3. Which code?" exam="NEET level"}
A scientist names a newly found species of frog. Which code of nomenclature does the name follow?

---

A frog is an animal, so the name follows the **ICZN** (International Code of Zoological Nomenclature). ICBN is for plants.
:::

## The hierarchy

:::question{title="4. Descending order" exam="NEET level"}
Which sequence of categories is in correct descending order (highest first)?

- (a) Kingdom → Class → Phylum → Family
- (b) Kingdom → Phylum → Order → Genus
- (c) Phylum → Kingdom → Order → Species
- (d) Class → Order → Phylum → Genus

---

**(b).** Kingdom is above phylum, phylum above order (class lies between them but is skipped, not misplaced), and order above genus. In (a), class is put above phylum; in (c), phylum is put above kingdom; in (d), phylum is put below order.
:::

:::question{title="5. Most in common" exam="NEET level"}
Members of which category share the **largest** number of common characters: genus, family, species or order?

---

**Species.** The lower the taxon, the more characters its members share. Common characters decrease from species up to kingdom.
:::

:::question{title="6. Counting taxa" exam="NEET level"}
Lion, tiger, leopard and the domestic cat are listed together. How many genera and how many families do they belong to?

---

Lion, tiger and leopard are in *Panthera*; the cat is in *Felis*. All are in Felidae. So **2 genera and 1 family**.
:::

:::question{title="7. Cat and dog" exam="NEET level"}
A cat and a dog belong to the same order but to different families. Name the order and the two families.

---

Order **Carnivora**. Cat: family **Felidae**. Dog: family **Canidae**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Mango and wheat" exam="NEET level"}
Using NCERT's Table 1.1, what is the **lowest** category that mango and wheat share?

---

Their families (Anacardiaceae, Poaceae), orders (Sapindales, Poales) and classes (Dicotyledonae, Monocotyledonae) all differ. Both are in **division Angiospermae**, so that is the lowest shared category.
:::

:::question{title="9. Man and housefly" exam="NEET level"}
Man is in phylum Chordata and the housefly in phylum Arthropoda. What is the lowest category they share?

---

Their phyla already differ, so every lower category differs too. They share only the **kingdom (Animalia)**.
:::

## What is living? (exam extra)

:::question{title="10. Defining feature" exam="NEET level"}
Which one is a defining property of living organisms, true of every living thing and of no non-living thing?
(a) Growth (b) Reproduction (c) Metabolism (d) Increase in mass

---

**(c) Metabolism.** Non-living things such as sand mounds also grow and gain mass (by material added on the surface), and mules or sterile worker bees do not reproduce.
:::
