All numbers below are NCERT's values for a healthy adult.

## What blood is made of

Blood is a **special connective tissue**: a fluid matrix (plasma) plus formed elements.

| Part | Share | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Plasma | about 55% of blood | Straw coloured, viscous |
| Formed elements | about 45% of blood | RBCs, WBCs, platelets |

:::formula{title="Plasma"}
- Water: **90–92%** of plasma.
- Proteins: **6–8%**. Fibrinogen (clotting), globulins (defence), albumins (osmotic balance).
- Small amounts of minerals: Na⁺, Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺, HCO₃⁻, Cl⁻.
- Glucose, amino acids, lipids (always in transit), and clotting factors in an **inactive** form.
:::

:::formula{title="Serum"}
$$\text{Serum} = \text{plasma} - \text{clotting factors}$$
:::

## Formed elements: the numbers

| Cell | Count per mm³ | Key fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RBC | 5–5.5 million (adult man) | Lives 120 days |
| WBC | 6000–8000 | Nucleated, short-lived |
| Platelets | 1.5–3.5 lakh | From megakaryocytes |

:::formula{title="Haemoglobin"}
**12–16 g** of haemoglobin in every **100 mL** of blood.

RBCs are made in the **red bone marrow** (adults) and destroyed in the **spleen** (the "graveyard of RBCs").
:::

:::formula{title="Kinds of WBC (share of all WBCs)"}
| WBC | Share | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Neutrophils | 60–65% | Phagocytic |
| Lymphocytes | 20–25% | B and T cells, immunity |
| Monocytes | 6–8% | Phagocytic |
| Eosinophils | 2–3% | Resist infection, allergy |
| Basophils | 0.5–1% | Histamine, serotonin, heparin |

Granulocytes: neutrophils, eosinophils, basophils. Agranulocytes: lymphocytes, monocytes.
:::

## Blood groups

:::formula{title="ABO groups (NCERT Table 15.1)"}
| Group | Antigens on RBCs | Antibodies in plasma | Donor's group |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | A | anti-B | A, O |
| B | B | anti-A | B, O |
| AB | A, B | nil | AB, A, B, O |
| O | nil | anti-A, anti-B | O |

"Donor's group" = the groups this person can safely receive blood from.

**O** = universal donor (no A or B antigen on its RBCs). **AB** = universal recipient (no anti-A or anti-B in its plasma).
:::

:::formula{title="Rh group"}
- **Rh+ve:** Rh antigen present on RBCs (nearly **80%** of humans).
- **Rh−ve:** Rh antigen absent. Makes anti-Rh antibodies only **after exposure** to Rh+ve blood.
- **Erythroblastosis foetalis:** Rh−ve mother, Rh+ve foetus, in a **later** pregnancy. Prevented by giving the mother **anti-Rh antibodies** just after the first delivery.
:::

## Clotting

:::formula{title="Coagulation chain"}
Injury → platelets (and injured tissue) release factors → cascade → **thrombokinase**.

$$\text{Prothrombin} \xrightarrow{\text{thrombokinase}} \text{thrombin}$$
$$\text{Fibrinogen} \xrightarrow{\text{thrombin}} \text{fibrin}$$

The clot is a network of fibrin threads with trapped dead and damaged formed elements. **Ca²⁺** ions are very important for clotting.
:::

## Heart rate and the cardiac cycle

:::formula{title="Heart rate"}
SAN fires **70–75** action potentials per minute, so the heart beats **70–75 times a minute**. NCERT's average: **72 beats/min**.
:::

:::formula{title="Duration of one cardiac cycle"}
$$t_{\text{cycle}} = \frac{60\ \text{s}}{\text{heart rate (per min)}}$$
At 72 beats/min, $60/72 \approx 0.83$ s. NCERT gives this as **0.8 s**: use 0.8 s in answers.
:::

:::formula{title="Phases of one cycle (beyond the syllabus)"}
Not in NCERT or the NEET syllabus. A common textbook split of a 0.8 s cycle, given only as an aid:

| Phase | Time |
| --- | --- |
| Atrial systole | 0.1 s |
| Ventricular systole | 0.3 s |
| Joint diastole (all four relaxed) | 0.4 s |

So atrial diastole lasts 0.7 s and ventricular diastole 0.5 s.
:::

:::formula{title="Cardiac output"}
$$\text{CO} = \text{SV} \times \text{HR}$$
CO = cardiac output, SV = stroke volume, HR = heart rate (per minute).
- **Stroke volume:** blood pumped by **each ventricle** in one cycle, about **70 mL**.
- **Cardiac output:** blood pumped by **each ventricle per minute**, about **5000 mL (5 L)**.
- Check: $70 \times 72 = 5040$ mL, about 5 L per minute.
- Unit: mL per minute (or L per minute).
:::

:::formula{title="Heart rate from an ECG"}
$$\text{HR} = \frac{N}{t}$$
HR = heart rate (beats per minute), $N$ = number of QRS complexes counted, $t$ = time of the strip in **minutes**. Each QRS complex is one heartbeat.
:::

## Heart sounds and ECG waves

| Event | Cause |
| --- | --- |
| First sound, **lub** | Tricuspid and bicuspid valves close |
| Second sound, **dub** | Semilunar valves close |

| ECG wave | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| P | Depolarisation of the atria (atria contract) |
| QRS | Depolarisation of the ventricles (systole starts shortly after Q) |
| T | Repolarisation of the ventricles (its end = end of systole) |

## Blood pressure

:::formula{title="Normal and high blood pressure"}
Normal: **120/80** mm Hg. 120 = systolic (pumping) pressure, 80 = diastolic (resting) pressure.

**Hypertension:** repeated readings of **140/90** mm Hg or higher.
:::
