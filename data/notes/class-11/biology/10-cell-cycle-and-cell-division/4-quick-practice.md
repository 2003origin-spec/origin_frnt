## Cell cycle

:::question{title="1. Counting through interphase" exam="NEET level"}
Onion root tip cells have 16 chromosomes. The DNA content of a cell just after M phase is $2C$. Give the chromosome number and DNA content in $G_1$, and in $G_2$.

---

S phase doubles the DNA but not the chromosome number. $G_1$: **16 chromosomes, $2C$**. $G_2$: **16 chromosomes, $4C$** (each chromosome now has 2 chromatids).
:::

:::question{title="2. Centriole" exam="NEET level"}
In an animal cell, in which phase of the cell cycle does the centriole duplicate, and where in the cell?

---

**In S phase, in the cytoplasm**, while the DNA replicates in the nucleus.
:::

:::question{title="3. Heart cells" exam="NEET level"}
Adult heart cells do not seem to divide. Which stage of the cell cycle are they in, and are they metabolically active?

---

They have left $G_1$ and are in the **quiescent stage ($G_0$)**. They **are metabolically active**; they just do not proliferate.
:::

## Mitosis

:::question{title="4. Name the stage" exam="NEET level"}
In a mitotic cell, chromosome condensation is complete, the chromosomes lie at the equator, and spindle fibres from opposite poles are attached to the kinetochores of the two sister chromatids. Name the stage.

---

**Metaphase.** It is also the best stage to study chromosome morphology.
:::

:::question{title="5. Anaphase count" exam="NEET level"}
A plant cell has $2n = 20$ and $2C$ DNA in $G_1$. How many chromosomes, and how much DNA, does it have at anaphase of mitosis?

---

The centromeres split, so the 20 chromosomes become 40 daughter chromosomes. No DNA is made or lost. Answer: **40 chromosomes and $4C$ DNA**.
:::

:::question{title="6. Coconut" exam="NEET level"}
The liquid endosperm of coconut has many free nuclei in one mass of cytoplasm. Which step of cell division did not happen, and what is such a structure called?

---

Karyokinesis happened but **cytokinesis did not**. The multinucleate structure is a **syncytium**.
:::

## Meiosis

:::question{title="7. Order of prophase I events" exam="NEET level"}
Put these events of prophase I in the correct order:
- (a) terminalisation of chiasmata
- (b) appearance of recombination nodules
- (c) synapsis
- (d) dissolution of the synaptonemal complex

---

Synapsis (zygotene), recombination nodules (pachytene), dissolution of the synaptonemal complex (diplotene), terminalisation (diakinesis): **c, b, d, a**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Two anaphases" exam="NEET level"}
In which anaphase, mitotic anaphase or anaphase I of meiosis, do sister chromatids stay joined at the centromere? What moves to the poles instead?

---

**Anaphase I.** The **homologous chromosomes** separate and move to opposite poles, each still made of two sister chromatids.
:::

:::question{title="9. Counting in meiosis" exam="NEET level"}
A meiocyte has $2n = 24$ and $2C$ DNA in $G_1$. Find (i) the number of bivalents at metaphase I, and (ii) the chromosome number and DNA content of each cell at metaphase II.

---

(i) Bivalents $= n =$ **12**.

(ii) Meiosis I halved the chromosome number, and the $4C$ DNA was shared between two cells. Each cell has **12 chromosomes (24 chromatids) and $2C$ DNA**.
:::

:::question{title="10. From gamete to root tip" exam="NEET level"}
The gametes of a plant have 7 chromosomes and $C$ DNA. How many chromosomes and chromatids does a root tip cell of this plant have at mitotic metaphase, and what is its DNA content?

---

Body cells are diploid: $2n = 14$. At metaphase each chromosome has 2 chromatids and the DNA has doubled. Answer: **14 chromosomes, 28 chromatids, $4C$ DNA**.
:::
