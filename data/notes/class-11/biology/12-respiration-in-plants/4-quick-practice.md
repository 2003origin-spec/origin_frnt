## Glycolysis and fermentation

:::question{title="1. Net gain of glycolysis" exam="NEET level"}
How many ATP molecules are gained (net) when one glucose molecule is broken down to pyruvic acid by glycolysis?

---

4 ATP are made (2 at BPGA → PGA, 2 at PEP → pyruvic acid) and 2 are used (glucose and fructose-6-phosphate steps). Net: **2 ATP**.
:::

:::question{title="2. The NADH step" exam="NEET level"}
At which step of glycolysis is $\ce{NAD+}$ reduced to $\ce{NADH + H+}$?

---

**3-phosphoglyceraldehyde (PGAL) → 1,3-bisphosphoglycerate (BPGA).** PGAL is oxidised and two hydrogen atoms pass to $\ce{NAD+}$.
:::

:::question{title="3. Yeast in a closed jar" exam="NEET level"}
Yeast ferments 2 molecules of glucose to ethanol with no oxygen present. How many molecules of $\ce{CO2}$ are released, and what is the net ATP gain?

---

Each glucose → 2 pyruvic acid → 2 ethanol + 2 $\ce{CO2}$, with a net 2 ATP. For 2 glucose: **4 $\ce{CO2}$ and 4 ATP**.
:::

## Aerobic respiration

:::question{title="4. Two turns of the cycle" exam="NEET level"}
One glucose gives 2 acetyl CoA. When both are oxidised in Krebs' cycle, how many NADH + H⁺, FADH₂ and ATP (via GTP) are made?

---

Each turn gives 3 NADH, 1 $\ce{FADH2}$ and 1 ATP. Two turns: **6 NADH + H⁺, 2 FADH₂ and 2 ATP**.
:::

:::question{title="5. One pyruvic acid" exam="NEET level"}
How many ATP are formed when one molecule of pyruvic acid is completely oxidised in the mitochondria? Use NCERT's summary equation for this phase.

---

The equation gives 4 NADH, 1 $\ce{FADH2}$ and 1 ATP per pyruvic acid. $4 \times 3 + 1 \times 2 + 1 = 15$. Answer: **15 ATP**.
:::

:::question{title="6. Entry point of FADH₂" exam="NEET level"}
Electrons from $\ce{FADH2}$ enter the electron transport system through which complex, and how many ATP does one $\ce{FADH2}$ give?

---

**Complex II** (succinate dehydrogenase), which passes them to ubiquinone. One $\ce{FADH2}$ gives **2 ATP**.
:::

:::question{title="7. Protons through F₀" exam="NEET level"}
How many protons must pass through the $F_0$ channel of ATP synthase to make 3 ATP, and in which direction do they move?

---

4 H⁺ per ATP, so $3 \times 4 =$ **12 H⁺**, moving **from the intermembrane space into the matrix**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Where the 38 ATP come from" exam="NEET level"}
Of the 38 ATP that NCERT counts for one glucose, how many are made by oxidative phosphorylation?

---

One glucose gives 10 NADH and 2 $\ce{FADH2}$ (counting glycolytic NADH, as NCERT assumes). $10 \times 3 + 2 \times 2 = 34$. The other 4 are made directly (2 net in glycolysis, 2 in Krebs' cycle). Answer: **34**.
:::

## Amphibolic pathway and RQ

:::question{title="9. Building fatty acids" exam="NEET level"}
A cell needs to make fatty acids. Which respiratory intermediate is withdrawn for this, and what name does this two-way role give the respiratory pathway?

---

**Acetyl CoA** is withdrawn. Because the pathway serves both breakdown and synthesis, it is an **amphibolic pathway**.
:::

:::question{title="10. Guess the substrate" exam="NEET level"}
Germinating seeds take in 50 mL of $\ce{O2}$ and give out 35 mL of $\ce{CO2}$ in the same time. Find the RQ. Which kind of food are they mainly respiring?

---

$\text{RQ} = \dfrac{35}{50} = 0.7$. An RQ of 0.7 (less than 1) matches **fats**, as in oilseeds. Answer: **RQ 0.7, fats**.
:::
