## Overall equations

:::formula{title="Overall equation (NCERT form)"}
$$\begin{aligned} & \ce{6CO2 + 12H2O} \\ & \ce{->[light] C6H12O6 + 6H2O + 6O2} \end{aligned}$$
All 6 $\ce{O2}$ come from **water**, not from $\ce{CO2}$. This was proved with radioisotopes.
:::

:::formula{title="Van Niel's general equation"}
$$\begin{aligned} & \ce{2H2A + CO2} \\ & \ce{->[light] 2A + CH2O + H2O} \end{aligned}$$
$\ce{H2A}$ = the hydrogen donor. Green plants: $\ce{H2A} = \ce{H2O}$, so A is oxygen. Purple and green sulphur bacteria: $\ce{H2A} = \ce{H2S}$, so the product is sulphur or sulphate, and **no** $\ce{O2}$ is given out.
:::

## Light reaction

:::formula{title="Splitting of water (at PS II)"}
$$\ce{2H2O -> 4H+ + O2 + 4e-}$$
Happens on the **inner (lumen) side** of the thylakoid membrane. The electrons replace those lost by PS II.
:::

:::formula{title="Reduction of NADP+"}
$$\begin{aligned} & \ce{NADP+ + 2e- + 2H+} \\ & \ce{-> NADPH + H+} \end{aligned}$$
Done by **NADP reductase**, on the **stroma side** of the membrane. The electrons come from the acceptor of PS I.
:::

| Photosystem | Reaction centre | Absorbs at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PS I | P700 (chlorophyll a) | 700 nm |
| PS II | P680 (chlorophyll a) | 680 nm |

PS I and PS II are numbered in the order they were **discovered**, not the order they work. In the Z scheme, PS II works first.

| | Non-cyclic | Cyclic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photosystems | PS II and PS I | PS I only |
| Electron path | Water → PS II → PS I → NADP+ | PS I → back to PS I |
| Products | ATP, NADPH, $\ce{O2}$ | ATP only |
| Where | Grana lamellae | Stroma lamellae (likely site) |

## Calvin cycle (C3 cycle)

:::formula{title="Carboxylation (first step)"}
$$\ce{RuBP + CO2 -> 2 PGA}$$
Enzyme: **RuBisCO**. RuBP is a 5-carbon ketose sugar, PGA (3-phosphoglyceric acid) is a 3-carbon acid. Carbon check: 5 + 1 = 2 × 3.
:::

:::formula{title="Energy cost per CO2 fixed"}
- Reduction: **2 ATP + 2 NADPH**
- Regeneration of RuBP: **1 ATP**
- Total: **3 ATP + 2 NADPH** per $\ce{CO2}$
:::

:::formula{title="Energy cost per glucose (6 turns)"}
| In | Out |
| --- | --- |
| 6 $\ce{CO2}$ | 1 glucose |
| 18 ATP | 18 ADP |
| 12 NADPH | 12 NADP+ |

For $n$ molecules of $\ce{CO2}$: $3n$ ATP and $2n$ NADPH.
:::

:::formula{title="C4 plants: extra ATP (exam extra)"}
Exam extra: the C4 pathway is in the NEET syllabus, but NCERT gives no ATP count for it.

Regenerating PEP from pyruvate in the mesophyll costs the equivalent of **2 more ATP per $\ce{CO2}$**.

So a C4 plant needs about **5 ATP + 2 NADPH** per $\ce{CO2}$, or 30 ATP + 12 NADPH per glucose. NCERT gives only the Calvin cycle numbers (3 ATP + 2 NADPH).
:::

## C4 (Hatch and Slack) pathway

:::formula{title="First fixation in C4 plants (mesophyll)"}
$$\ce{PEP + CO2 -> OAA}$$
Enzyme: **PEP carboxylase (PEPcase)**. PEP (phosphoenol pyruvate) has 3 carbons, OAA (oxaloacetic acid) has 4. NCERT's figure shows $\ce{CO2}$ entering as $\ce{HCO3-}$.
:::

Steps in order:
1. Mesophyll: PEP + $\ce{CO2}$ → OAA (by PEPcase).
2. Mesophyll: OAA → other C4 acids (malic acid or aspartic acid).
3. C4 acid moves to the **bundle sheath** cells.
4. Bundle sheath: C4 acid → $\ce{CO2}$ + a 3-carbon molecule.
5. $\ce{CO2}$ enters the Calvin cycle (RuBisCO) in the bundle sheath.
6. The 3-carbon molecule goes back to the mesophyll and is turned into PEP again.

## Photorespiration

:::formula{title="Oxygenation by RuBisCO"}
$$\begin{aligned} & \ce{RuBP + O2} \\ & \ce{-> PGA + phosphoglycolate} \end{aligned}$$
PGA has 3 carbons and phosphoglycolate has 2. No sugar, no ATP and no NADPH are made. Later steps of the photorespiratory pathway release $\ce{CO2}$ and use up ATP.
:::

## C3 vs C4 plants

NCERT's Table 11.1 is left blank for you to fill in. This table gives the usual answers.

| Feature | C3 | C4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First $\ce{CO2}$ acceptor | RuBP (5C) | PEP (3C) |
| First stable product | PGA (3C) | OAA (4C) |
| First fixing enzyme | RuBisCO | PEPcase |
| First carboxylation in | Mesophyll | Mesophyll |
| Cell types that fix $\ce{CO2}$ | One: mesophyll | Two: mesophyll and bundle sheath |
| Calvin cycle in | Mesophyll | Bundle sheath only |
| Has RuBisCO? | Yes, in mesophyll | Yes, in bundle sheath only |
| PEPcase (for fixation) | No | Yes, in mesophyll |
| Kranz anatomy | Absent | Present |
| $\ce{CO2}$ fixation rate in high light | Low | High |
| Photorespiration at high light or low $\ce{CO2}$ | High | Negligible |
| Photorespiration at high $\ce{CO2}$ | Negligible | Negligible |
| Temperature optimum | 20–25°C | 30–40°C |
| $\ce{CO2}$ saturation | Above 450 µL/L | About 360 µL/L |
| Biomass productivity | Lower | Higher |
| Examples | Tomato, bell pepper | Maize, sorghum |
