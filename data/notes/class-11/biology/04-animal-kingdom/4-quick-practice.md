## Basis of classification

:::question{title="1. A false body cavity" exam="NEET level"}
In one phylum the body cavity is not lined by mesoderm. Instead, the mesoderm is present as scattered pouches between the ectoderm and endoderm. Name the phylum and the kind of body cavity.

---

**Aschelminthes**, which have a **pseudocoelom** (they are pseudocoelomates).
:::

:::question{title="2. Odd one out" exam="NEET level"}
Which one of these is **not** triploblastic: *Fasciola*, *Ascaris*, *Adamsia*, *Pheretima*?

---

***Adamsia*** (sea anemone). It is a coelenterate, so it is diploblastic. The other three are a flatworm, a roundworm and an annelid, all triploblastic.
:::

:::question{title="3. Comb plates" exam="NEET level"}
A marine animal is radially symmetrical and diploblastic. It has eight rows of ciliated plates, glows in the dark and reproduces only sexually. Name its phylum.

---

**Ctenophora** (comb jellies), for example *Pleurobrachia*. The comb plates and bioluminescence rule out Coelenterata.
:::

## Non-chordates

:::question{title="4. Excretory structures" exam="NEET level"}
Name the phylum that has each of these: (a) flame cells, (b) nephridia, (c) Malpighian tubules, (d) proboscis gland.

---

**(a) Platyhelminthes, (b) Annelida, (c) Arthropoda, (d) Hemichordata.**
:::

:::question{title="5. Open circulation" exam="NEET level"}
Of Annelida, Arthropoda, Hemichordata and Chordata, which two have an **open** circulatory system?

---

**Arthropoda and Hemichordata.** Annelids and chordates have a closed system.
:::

:::question{title="6. Metagenesis" exam="NEET level"}
In *Obelia*, which body form produces the other asexually?

---

**The polyp.** Polyps produce medusae asexually, and medusae produce polyps sexually.
:::

:::question{title="7. Spiny skin" exam="NEET level"}
A marine animal has an endoskeleton of calcareous ossicles, a water vascular system, no excretory system and a bilateral larva. Where are the mouth and anus of the adult?

---

It is an echinoderm. The **mouth is on the lower (ventral) side** and the **anus on the upper (dorsal) side**.
:::

## Hemichordates and chordates

:::question{title="8. Not quite a notochord" exam="NEET level"}
*Balanoglossus* has a rudimentary structure in its collar region that looks like a notochord. Name the structure and the phylum of *Balanoglossus*.

---

The **stomochord**. *Balanoglossus* belongs to **Hemichordata**, which is now placed under the non-chordates.
:::

:::question{title="9. Which fish?" exam="NEET level"}
A fish has an operculum over its gills and an air bladder. Which of these could it be: *Scoliodon*, *Pristis*, *Catla*, *Trygon*?

---

***Catla***, a bony fish (Osteichthyes). The other three are cartilaginous fishes, which have no operculum and no air bladder.
:::

:::question{title="10. Three chambers, cold blood" exam="NEET level"}
From *Crocodilus*, *Rana*, *Calotes*, *Columba* and *Clarias*, pick the animals that are cold-blooded **and** have a three-chambered heart.

---

***Rana* and *Calotes*.** Amphibians have a 3-chambered heart, and so do most reptiles. *Crocodilus* is the reptile exception with 4 chambers, *Columba* (a bird) is warm-blooded with 4, and *Clarias* (a bony fish) has 2.
:::
