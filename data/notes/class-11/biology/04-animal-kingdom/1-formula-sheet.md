## The basis of classification

:::formula{title="Levels of organisation"}
- **Cellular:** loose cell aggregates. Porifera (sponges).
- **Tissue:** cells doing one job form tissues. Coelenterata and Ctenophora.
- **Organ:** tissues form organs. Platyhelminthes.
- **Organ system:** organs form systems. Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, Echinodermata, Chordata (and Aschelminthes, Hemichordata).
:::

:::formula{title="Symmetry"}
- **Asymmetrical:** no plane through the centre gives equal halves. Most sponges.
- **Radial:** any plane through the central axis gives two identical halves. Coelenterates, ctenophores, adult echinoderms.
- **Bilateral:** only **one** plane gives identical left and right halves. Platyhelminthes onwards (annelids, arthropods and others), except adult echinoderms.
:::

:::formula{title="Germ layers"}
- **Diploblastic:** ectoderm + endoderm, with the undifferentiated **mesoglea** between them. Coelenterates and ctenophores.
- **Triploblastic:** a third layer, **mesoderm**, between them. Platyhelminthes to Chordata.
:::

:::formula{title="Body cavity"}
- **Coelomate:** cavity lined by mesoderm (true coelom). Annelida, Arthropoda, Mollusca, Echinodermata, Hemichordata, Chordata.
- **Pseudocoelomate:** mesoderm only as scattered pouches between ectoderm and endoderm. Aschelminthes.
- **Acoelomate:** no body cavity. Platyhelminthes (Figure 4.4 also puts sponges, cnidarians and ctenophores here).
:::

## Phylum Chordata

:::formula{title="Three subphyla"}
- **Urochordata (Tunicata):** notochord only in the **larval tail**. *Ascidia*, *Salpa*, *Doliolum*.
- **Cephalochordata:** notochord from **head to tail**, for the whole life. *Branchiostoma* (Amphioxus).
- **Vertebrata:** notochord in the embryo, replaced by a vertebral column in the adult (but it persists for life in Chondrichthyes).

The first two are the **protochordates**, and they are exclusively marine.
:::

:::formula{title="Subphylum Vertebrata"}
- **Agnatha** (no jaws): class Cyclostomata.
- **Gnathostomata** (jaws):
  - superclass **Pisces** (fins): Chondrichthyes, Osteichthyes;
  - superclass **Tetrapoda** (limbs): Amphibia, Reptilia, Aves, Mammalia.
:::

## The vertebrate classes

| Class | Heart chambers | Body temperature |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chondrichthyes | 2 (1 auricle, 1 ventricle) | Cold-blooded |
| Osteichthyes | 2 (1 auricle, 1 ventricle) | Cold-blooded |
| Amphibia | 3 (2 auricles, 1 ventricle) | Cold-blooded |
| Reptilia | Usually 3; 4 in crocodiles | Cold-blooded |
| Aves | 4 | Warm-blooded |
| Mammalia | 4 | Warm-blooded |

| Class | Fertilisation | Eggs or young |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chondrichthyes | Internal | Many viviparous |
| Osteichthyes | Usually external | Mostly oviparous |
| Amphibia | External | Oviparous |
| Reptilia | Internal | Oviparous |
| Aves | Internal | Oviparous |
| Mammalia | Internal | Viviparous (few exceptions) |

:::formula{title="Direct or indirect development"}
- **Indirect** (a larva): Amphibia, the only vertebrate class NCERT calls indirect (cyclostome larvae also metamorphose); also sponges, ctenophores, flatworms, molluscs, echinoderms, hemichordates.
- **Direct:** bony fishes, reptiles, birds and mammals.
- **Either:** roundworms and arthropods.
:::
