The current NCERT chapter has only two parts: **organ and organ system**, and the **frog**. Everything on this sheet is from that chapter unless it is labelled.

## Levels of organisation

:::formula{title="Cell to organism"}
Cells → tissues → organs → organ systems → organism.

- **Tissue:** a group of similar cells, along with intercellular substances, that performs a specific function.
- **Organ:** tissues arranged in a specific proportion and pattern (stomach, lung, heart, kidney).
- **Organ system:** two or more organs that do a common job by physical and/or chemical interaction (digestive system, respiratory system).
- Splitting the work this way shows **division of labour**.
:::

:::formula{title="Four basic animal tissues"}
All complex animals have only **four** basic types: **epithelial, connective, muscular and neural**.

The **heart** contains all four.
:::

:::formula{title="Morphology and anatomy"}
- **Morphology:** study of form, the externally visible features. In animals, the external appearance of the organs or parts of the body.
- **Anatomy:** in animals, the study of the morphology of the **internal** organs.
:::

## Frog at a glance

| Feature | Frog |
| --- | --- |
| Phylum, class | Chordata, Amphibia |
| Common Indian species | *Rana tigrina* (Indian bullfrog) |
| Body temperature | Varies with surroundings (poikilotherm) |
| Body parts | Head and trunk; **no neck, no tail** |
| Digits | Fore limb **4**, hind limb **5** (webbed) |
| Heart | 3 chambers: 2 atria, 1 ventricle |
| Blood vascular system | Closed |
| RBCs | Nucleated, with haemoglobin |
| Nitrogenous waste | Urea (**ureotelic**) |
| Cranial nerves | **10 pairs** |
| Fertilisation | External, in water |
| Development | Through a tadpole larva (metamorphosis) |

:::formula{title="Summer and winter sleep"}
- **Aestivation:** summer sleep.
- **Hibernation:** winter sleep.

In both, the frog shelters in deep burrows, and gas exchange takes place through the **skin**.
:::

## Organ systems

:::formula{title="Path of food"}
Mouth → buccal cavity → pharynx → oesophagus → stomach → intestine → rectum → cloaca.

The first part of the small intestine is the **duodenum**.
:::

| Gland | Secretes | Reaches the duodenum by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Liver | Bile (stored in the gall bladder) | Common bile duct |
| Pancreas | Pancreatic juice | Common bile duct |

:::formula{title="Two portal systems"}
- **Hepatic portal system:** special venous link between the **intestine and the liver**.
- **Renal portal system:** special venous link between the **kidneys and the lower parts of the body**.
:::

:::formula{title="Heart chambers and extra sacs"}
- **Sinus venosus:** triangular; joins the **right atrium**; receives blood from the venae cavae.
- **Conus arteriosus:** sac-like; the **ventricle** opens into it, on the **ventral** side of the heart.
- **Pericardium:** the membrane covering the heart.
:::

## Brain

| Part | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Fore-brain | Olfactory lobes, paired cerebral hemispheres, unpaired diencephalon |
| Mid-brain | A pair of optic lobes |
| Hind-brain | Cerebellum, medulla oblongata |

:::formula{title="Nervous system"}
- **Central:** brain (in the cranium, or brain box) and spinal cord (in the vertebral column).
- **Peripheral:** cranial nerves (10 pairs) and spinal nerves.
- **Autonomic:** sympathetic and parasympathetic.

The medulla oblongata leaves the skull through the **foramen magnum** and continues as the spinal cord.
:::

:::formula{title="Endocrine glands of the frog"}
Pituitary, thyroid, parathyroid, thymus, pineal body, pancreatic islets, adrenals and gonads.
:::

## Sense organs

| Sense | Organ |
| --- | --- |
| Touch | Sensory papillae |
| Taste | Taste buds |
| Smell | Nasal epithelium |
| Vision | Eyes |
| Hearing | Tympanum with internal ears |

Only the **eyes** and **internal ears** are well-organised organs. The others are cells grouped around nerve endings.

## Male and female

| Feature | Male | Female |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Vocal sacs | Present | Absent |
| Copulatory pad (1st digit of fore limb) | Present | Absent |
| Gonads | Testes, yellowish, ovoid | Ovaries |
| Link to kidneys | Testes joined to kidneys | No functional link |
| Ureter | Urinogenital duct | Urine only |

:::formula{title="Path of sperm (male)"}
Testis → vasa efferentia (10–12) → kidney → Bidder's canal → urinogenital duct → cloaca.
:::

:::formula{title="Numbers to remember"}
- 2 atria + 1 ventricle = 3 chambers
- 10 pairs of cranial nerves
- 10–12 vasa efferentia
- 4 digits (fore limb), 5 digits (hind limb)
- 2500–3000 ova laid at a time
:::

## Animal tissues (exam extra)

Exam extra: "Animal tissues" is in the NEET syllabus, but the current NCERT chapter only names the four types (its summary adds that epithelia line surfaces, have one free surface and are joined at junctions). The jobs and examples below are not in the current book.

| Tissue | Main job | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Epithelial | Covers and lines | Skin, lining of gut |
| Connective | Links and supports | Bone, cartilage, blood |
| Muscular | Contracts | Biceps, heart wall |
| Neural | Carries messages | Brain, nerves |
