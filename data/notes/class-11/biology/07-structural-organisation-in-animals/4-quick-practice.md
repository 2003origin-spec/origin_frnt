## Organisation and external features

:::question{title="1. Tissues in the heart" exam="NEET level"}
How many of the four basic animal tissue types (epithelial, connective, muscular, neural) are present in the heart?

---

**All four.** NCERT uses the heart as its example of an organ made of every basic tissue type.
:::

:::question{title="2. Telling the sexes apart" exam="NEET level"}
A student must sort adult frogs into males and females using only external features. Which two features mark a male?

---

**Vocal sacs** and a **copulatory pad on the first digit of each fore limb**. Both are absent in females.
:::

:::question{title="3. Counting digits" exam="NEET level"}
How many digits does an adult frog have on all four limbs together?

---

Each fore limb has 4 digits and each hind limb has 5. So $4 + 4 + 5 + 5 =$ **18**.
:::

## Organ systems

:::question{title="4. Breathing in winter" exam="NEET level"}
A frog is hibernating in a deep burrow. Through which organ does gas exchange take place?

---

**The skin** (cutaneous respiration). NCERT: during aestivation and hibernation, gas exchange takes place through the skin.
:::

:::question{title="5. Portal systems" exam="NEET level"}
Name the special venous connection in the frog between the kidneys and the lower parts of the body.

---

**The renal portal system.** The link between the liver and the intestine is the hepatic portal system.
:::

:::question{title="6. Into the duodenum" exam="NEET level"}
Through which duct do bile and pancreatic juice reach the frog's duodenum, and what does the bile do there?

---

Both arrive through the **common bile duct**. Bile **emulsifies fat**; pancreatic juice digests carbohydrates and proteins.
:::

:::question{title="7. Path of sperms" exam="NEET level"}
Put these in the order in which sperms pass through them: cloaca, Bidder's canal, testis, urinogenital duct, vasa efferentia.

---

**Testis → vasa efferentia → Bidder's canal → urinogenital duct → cloaca.** The vasa efferentia enter the kidney and open into Bidder's canal, which joins the urinogenital duct.
:::

:::question{title="8. Odd one out in the brain" exam="NEET level"}
Which of these is **not** part of the frog's fore-brain: olfactory lobes, cerebral hemispheres, diencephalon, optic lobes?

---

**Optic lobes.** They belong to the mid-brain. The other three make up the fore-brain.
:::

## Mixed

:::question{title="9. How many are correct?" exam="NEET level"}
How many of these statements about the frog are correct?

1. Its RBCs are nucleated.
2. In the female, the ureters and oviducts open separately into the cloaca.
3. It has an external ear.
4. Its eyes are compound eyes.
5. It excretes mainly urea.

---

Statements 1, 2 and 5 are correct. The frog has **no external ear** (only the tympanum shows), and its eyes are **simple** (one unit each). So **3** are correct.
:::

:::question{title="10. Cell junctions" exam="NEET level"}
Exam extra topic (animal tissues, in the NEET syllabus). Which type of cell junction joins the cytoplasm of neighbouring cells, so that ions and small molecules pass quickly between them?

---

**Gap junctions.** Tight junctions stop substances leaking across a tissue, and adhering junctions cement cells together.
:::
