## Leaves and inflorescence

:::question{title="1. Leaf or branch?" exam="NEET level"}
A green structure has many small blades on a common axis. There are no buds in the axils of the small blades, but there is one bud where the axis joins the stem. What is the structure?

---

**A pinnately compound leaf** (as in neem). The small blades are leaflets on the rachis. Buds occur in the axil of the petiole, never in the axil of a leaflet.
:::

:::question{title="2. Same phyllotaxy" exam="NEET level"}
Which two plants have the same phyllotaxy?
(a) Guava and china rose (b) *Calotropis* and guava (c) *Alstonia* and mustard (d) Sunflower and *Calotropis*

---

**(b).** *Calotropis* and guava both have opposite phyllotaxy. China rose, mustard and sunflower are alternate; *Alstonia* is whorled.
:::

:::question{title="3. Inflorescence type" exam="NEET level"}
In an inflorescence the main axis ends in a flower, and the oldest flower is at the top. Name the type and the order of the flowers.

---

**Cymose, with flowers in basipetal order.** The axis stops growing because its tip becomes a flower.
:::

## The flower

:::question{title="4. Inferior ovary" exam="NEET level"}
Which flower has an inferior ovary?
(a) Mustard (b) Plum (c) Cucumber (d) Brinjal

---

**(c) Cucumber.** It is epigynous. Mustard and brinjal are hypogynous (superior ovary); plum is perigynous (half-inferior).
:::

:::question{title="5. Aestivation" exam="NEET level"}
In the bud, the petal margins overlap one another, but not in any particular direction. This is seen in:
(a) *Calotropis* (b) China rose (c) Gulmohur (d) Pea

---

**(c) Gulmohur** (imbricate). *Calotropis* is valvate, china rose is twisted, and pea is vexillary.
:::

:::question{title="6. Counting pollen sacs" exam="NEET level"}
A mustard flower has 6 stamens. Each anther is a typical bilobed anther. How many pollen sacs does the flower have?

---

Each anther has 2 lobes × 2 chambers = 4 pollen sacs. So $6 \times 4 =$ **24 pollen sacs**.
:::

:::question{title="7. Placentation" exam="NEET level"}
An ovary is one-chambered but becomes two-chambered because of a false septum. The ovules are on the inner wall. Name the placentation and one NCERT example.

---

**Parietal placentation**, as in **mustard** (or *Argemone*).
:::

## Floral formula and seeds

:::question{title="8. Reading a formula" exam="NEET level"}
A flower has the formula ⊕ ⚥ $K_{(5)}\ \overgroup{C_{(5)}\,A_5}\ \underline{G}_{(2)}$. Which statement about it is **wrong**?
- (a) The stamens are attached to the petals.
- (b) The ovary is inferior.
- (c) The sepals are united.
- (d) The gynoecium has two fused carpels.

---

**(b) is wrong.** The line is **below** G, so the ovary is **superior**. The arc over C and A shows epipetalous stamens. This is the Solanaceae formula.
:::

:::question{title="9. Endosperm" exam="NEET level"}
Which pair is **wrongly** matched?
- (a) Castor: endospermic seed
- (b) Orchid: endospermic seed
- (c) Pea: non-endospermous seed
- (d) Maize: endospermic seed

---

**(b).** Most monocot seeds are endospermic, but orchid seeds are **non-endospermic**.
:::

:::question{title="10. Maize seed" exam="NEET level"}
In a maize seed, name (i) the shield-shaped cotyledon and (ii) the sheath that covers the radicle.

---

**(i) Scutellum. (ii) Coleorhiza.** The plumule is covered by the coleoptile.
:::
