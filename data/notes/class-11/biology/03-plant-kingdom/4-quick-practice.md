## Algae

:::question{title="1. Name the class" exam="NEET level"}
A marine alga has chlorophyll a and d, stores floridean starch and makes no flagellated cells at any stage. To which class does it belong?

---

**Rhodophyceae (red algae).** Chlorophyll d, floridean starch and the absence of flagella are all red-algal features.
:::

:::question{title="2. Kinds of gamete fusion" exam="NEET level"}
Match each alga with its type of sexual reproduction: *Spirogyra*, *Eudorina*, *Fucus*. Choose from isogamy, anisogamy and oogamy.

---

***Spirogyra*: isogamy** (same-size gametes, but non-flagellated). ***Eudorina*: anisogamy** (gametes of different sizes). ***Fucus*: oogamy** (a large non-motile egg and a small motile male gamete).
:::

:::question{title="3. Algal products" exam="NEET level"}
Which of these is obtained from **brown** algae: agar, algin or carrageen?

---

**Algin.** Carrageen comes from red algae, and agar comes from *Gelidium* and *Gracilaria*, which are also red algae.
:::

## Bryophytes

:::question{title="4. Moss stages" exam="NEET level"}
In a moss, which stage grows directly from a spore? From what does the leafy stage arise, and which stage bears the sex organs?

---

The **protonema** grows from the spore. The leafy stage arises as a **lateral bud on the secondary protonema**. The **leafy stage** bears the antheridia and archegonia.
:::

:::question{title="5. Count the diploid cells" exam="NEET level"}
How many of these are diploid (2n)?
(i) protonema cell of a moss, (ii) cell of the seta of a moss, (iii) gemma cell of *Marchantia*, (iv) prothallus cell of a fern, (v) zygote of a fern, (vi) egg of a liverwort.

---

The seta is part of the sporophyte (2n), and a zygote is 2n. The protonema, gemma, prothallus and egg all belong to the gametophyte (n). So **2** are diploid: (ii) and (v).
:::

:::question{title="6. Gemmae" exam="NEET level"}
What are gemmae in *Marchantia*, where do they form, and what kind of reproduction do they carry out?

---

Gemmae are **green, multicellular buds** that form in **gemma cups** on the thallus. They fall off and grow into new plants, so they carry out **asexual reproduction**.
:::

## Pteridophytes and gymnosperms

:::question{title="7. Heterospory" exam="NEET level"}
From *Selaginella*, *Equisetum*, *Salvinia*, *Dryopteris* and *Adiantum*, pick the heterosporous genera.

---

***Selaginella* and *Salvinia***. They make large megaspores and small microspores. The others make one kind of spore.
:::

:::question{title="8. Pteridophyte classes" exam="NEET level"}
Match each genus with its class: *Psilotum*, *Lycopodium*, *Equisetum*, *Adiantum*. Classes: Lycopsida, Pteropsida, Psilopsida, Sphenopsida.

---

***Psilotum*: Psilopsida. *Lycopodium*: Lycopsida. *Equisetum*: Sphenopsida. *Adiantum*: Pteropsida.**
:::

:::question{title="9. Which gymnosperm?" exam="NEET level"}
A gymnosperm has coralloid roots, and its male cones and megasporophylls grow on separate trees. Name the plant, and the organisms living in its coralloid roots.

---

***Cycas***, with **nitrogen-fixing cyanobacteria** in the coralloid roots. (*Pinus* has fungal mycorrhiza instead, and bears both kinds of cones on the same tree.)
:::

## Whole chapter

:::question{title="10. Count the correct statements" exam="NEET level"}
How many of these statements are correct?
(a) Cyanobacteria are placed among the algae in Kingdom Plantae.
(b) The sporophyte of a moss depends on the gametophyte for food.
(c) Pteridophytes are the first land plants with xylem and phloem.
(d) In gymnosperms, the ovules are enclosed in an ovary.
(e) The female gametophyte of a gymnosperm is free-living.

---

(a) is wrong: cyanobacteria are no longer algae. (b) and (c) are correct. (d) is wrong: gymnosperms have no ovary, so the seeds are naked. (e) is wrong: the female gametophyte stays inside the ovule. **2** statements are correct.
:::
