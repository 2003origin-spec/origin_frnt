## The five groups at a glance

NCERT places five groups in Plantae: algae, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms and angiosperms.

| Group | Main plant body | Vascular tissue | Seeds |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Algae | Thallus | No | No |
| Bryophytes | Gametophyte (n) | No | No |
| Pteridophytes | Sporophyte (2n) | Yes | No |
| Gymnosperms | Sporophyte (2n) | Yes | Naked |
| Angiosperms | Sporophyte (2n) | Yes | In fruits |

:::formula{title="Who needs water for fertilisation?"}
- **Bryophytes and pteridophytes:** yes. Their male gametes (antherozoids) swim through water to the archegonium.
- **Gymnosperms:** no. A **pollen tube** carries the male gametes to the archegonia.
- **Angiosperms:** no. Pollen grains and ovules develop in **flowers**, and a pollen tube carries the male gametes (details in Class 12, Chapter 1).
:::

## Algae: the three classes (NCERT Table 3.1)

:::formula{title="Pigments"}
- **Chlorophyceae** (green): chlorophyll **a, b**
- **Phaeophyceae** (brown): chlorophyll **a, c**, fucoxanthin
- **Rhodophyceae** (red): chlorophyll **a, d**, phycoerythrin
:::

:::formula{title="Stored food"}
- Green: **starch** (in pyrenoids; some store oil droplets)
- Brown: **mannitol, laminarin**
- Red: **floridean starch** (similar to amylopectin and glycogen)
:::

:::formula{title="Cell wall"}
- Green: cellulose (inner layer of cellulose, outer layer of **pectose**)
- Brown: cellulose and **algin**
- Red: cellulose, pectin and **polysulphate esters**
:::

:::formula{title="Flagella (number and position)"}
- Green: **2–8, equal, apical**
- Brown: **2, unequal, lateral** (pear-shaped zoospores and gametes)
- Red: **absent** (spores and gametes are non-motile)
:::

| Class | Habitat | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Green | Fresh, brackish and salt water | *Chlamydomonas*, *Volvox*, *Ulothrix*, *Spirogyra*, *Chara* |
| Brown | Mostly marine; fresh water rare | *Ectocarpus*, *Dictyota*, *Laminaria*, *Sargassum*, *Fucus* |
| Red | Mostly marine (some fresh water); more in warm seas | *Polysiphonia*, *Porphyra*, *Gracilaria*, *Gelidium* |

## Kinds of sexual reproduction in algae

:::formula{title="Isogamy, anisogamy, oogamy"}
| Type | Gametes | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isogamy | Same size, flagellated | *Ulothrix* |
| Isogamy | Same size, non-flagellated | *Spirogyra* |
| Anisogamy | Different sizes | *Eudorina* |
| Oogamy | Large non-motile egg + small motile male | *Volvox*, *Fucus* |
:::

## Bryophytes: liverworts and mosses

:::formula{title="Liverwort vs moss"}
| | Liverwort | Moss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Thalloid, dorsiventral (*Marchantia*); leafy ones have 2 rows of tiny leaf-like parts | Upright axis with spirally arranged leaves |
| First stage from spore | Not described in NCERT | **Protonema** (green, branched, filament-like) |
| Asexual / vegetative | Fragmentation; **gemmae** in gemma cups | Fragmentation; **budding** in secondary protonema |
| Example | *Marchantia* | *Funaria*, *Polytrichum*, *Sphagnum* |
:::

:::formula{title="Bryophyte sex organs"}
- **Antheridium** (male): makes **biflagellate** antherozoids.
- **Archegonium** (female): **flask-shaped**, makes a **single egg**.
- Sporophyte = **foot + seta + capsule**. Meiosis in the capsule makes haploid spores.
:::

## Pteridophytes

:::formula{title="Four classes with NCERT examples"}
| Class | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Psilopsida | *Psilotum* |
| Lycopsida | *Selaginella*, *Lycopodium* |
| Sphenopsida | *Equisetum* (horsetail) |
| Pteropsida | *Dryopteris*, *Pteris*, *Adiantum* (ferns) |
:::

:::formula{title="Homosporous vs heterosporous"}
- **Homosporous:** one kind of spore. Most pteridophytes.
- **Heterosporous:** two kinds, **megaspores** (large, give female gametophytes) and **microspores** (small, give male gametophytes). Pteridophyte examples: ***Selaginella***, ***Salvinia***. All gymnosperms are heterosporous.
:::

:::formula{title="Leaves and cones"}
- **Microphylls** (small leaves): *Selaginella*. **Macrophylls** (large leaves): ferns.
- **Sporophyll:** a leaf-like part that bears sporangia.
- **Strobilus (cone):** compact group of sporophylls, e.g. *Selaginella*, *Equisetum*.
- **Prothallus:** the small, free-living, thalloid gametophyte of a pteridophyte.
:::

## Gymnosperms

:::formula{title="Roots of gymnosperms"}
- *Pinus*: fungal association, **mycorrhiza**.
- *Cycas*: **coralloid roots** with $\text{N}_2$-fixing **cyanobacteria**.
:::

:::formula{title="Male and female cones"}
| | Male (microsporangiate) | Female (macrosporangiate) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bears | Microsporophylls, microsporangia | Megasporophylls with ovules |
| Gives | Pollen grain (male gametophyte) | Female gametophyte with 2 or more archegonia |
:::

:::formula{title="Same tree or different trees?"}
- *Pinus*: male and female cones on the **same** tree.
- *Cycas*: male cones and megasporophylls on **different** trees.
:::

## Ploidy (chromosome sets)

:::formula{title="Haploid (n) or diploid (2n)?"}
| n (gametophyte or its products) | 2n (sporophyte) |
| --- | --- |
| Moss protonema, moss leaf, gemma | Moss seta and capsule wall |
| Fern prothallus, spores | Zygote, fern leaf |
| Antherozoid, egg, pollen grain | Spore mother cell |

Rule: **spores and everything grown from them are n** (the gametophyte and its gametes). **The zygote and everything grown from it are 2n** (the sporophyte). Meiosis in a 2n spore mother cell gives four n spores. One exception you will meet: the angiosperm endosperm is 3n (Class 12, Chapter 1).
:::

## Classification systems

| System | Based on | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artificial | A few visible characters (habit, leaves, androecium) | Linnaeus |
| Natural | Natural affinities: external and internal features | Bentham and Hooker |
| Phylogenetic | Evolutionary relationships; common ancestor | Accepted today |
