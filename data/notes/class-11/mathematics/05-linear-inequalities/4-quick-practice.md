## One inequality

:::question{title="1. Greatest integer" exam="Board level"}
Find the greatest integer $x$ that satisfies $-4x > 18$.

---

Divide by $-4$ and flip: $x < -4.5$. The integers less than $-4.5$ are $-5, -6, \dots$

The greatest is **−5**.
:::

:::question{title="2. Natural numbers" exam="Board level"}
Solve $7x - 5 \le 3x + 19$ when $x$ is a natural number.

---

$4x \le 24$, so $x \le 6$. Natural numbers start at 1.

Solution set: **{1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6}**.
:::

:::question{title="3. With fractions" exam="Board level"}
Solve $\dfrac{2x - 1}{3} - \dfrac{x + 2}{4} \ge 1$.

---

Multiply by 12 (positive, no flip): $4(2x - 1) - 3(x + 2) \ge 12$.

$8x - 4 - 3x - 6 \ge 12$, so $5x \ge 22$ and $x \ge \dfrac{22}{5}$.

Answer: **$x \in \left[\dfrac{22}{5}, \infty\right)$**.
:::

:::question{title="4. Brackets and a minus sign" exam="Board level"}
Solve $3 - 2(x - 4) > 5x - 3$.

---

$3 - 2x + 8 > 5x - 3$, so $11 - 2x > 5x - 3$.

$14 > 7x$, so $x < 2$. Answer: **$(-\infty, 2)$**.
:::

## Double inequalities and systems

:::question{title="5. A chain" exam="Board level"}
Solve $-3 < \dfrac{4 - 3x}{2} \le 5$.

---

Times 2: $-6 < 4 - 3x \le 10$. Minus 4: $-10 < -3x \le 6$.

Divide by $-3$ and flip both signs: $\dfrac{10}{3} > x \ge -2$.

Answer: **$-2 \le x < \dfrac{10}{3}$**, that is $\left[-2, \dfrac{10}{3}\right)$.
:::

:::question{title="6. A system" exam="Board level"}
Solve the system $2(x - 3) < x + 1$ and $5 - 3x \le 2x - 10$. How many integers satisfy it?

---

First: $2x - 6 < x + 1$, so $x < 7$. Second: $15 \le 5x$, so $x \ge 3$.

Common part: $3 \le x < 7$, that is $[3, 7)$. On a number line: dark circle at 3, open circle at 7.

The integers are 3, 4, 5, 6: **4 integers**.
:::

:::question{title="7. Any solution?" exam="Board level"}
Solve the system $x + 4 > 2x + 1$ and $3x - 2 \ge 2x + 5$.

---

First: $3 > x$, so $x < 3$. Second: $x \ge 7$.

No number is both less than 3 and at least 7. The system has **no solution** (empty set).
:::

## Word problems

:::question{title="8. Average marks" exam="Board level"}
Asha scored 72, 65 and 80 in three tests. What is the least score she needs in the fourth test to have an average of at least 75?

---

$\dfrac{72 + 65 + 80 + x}{4} \ge 75$, so $217 + x \ge 300$ and $x \ge 83$.

She needs at least **83** marks.
:::

:::question{title="9. Consecutive even numbers" exam="Board level"}
Find all pairs of consecutive even positive integers, both larger than 8, whose sum is less than 30.

---

Let the smaller be $x$; the other is $x + 2$. Then $x > 8$ and $2x + 2 < 30$, so $x < 14$.

Even $x$ with $8 < x < 14$: 10 and 12. Pairs: **(10, 12) and (12, 14)**.
:::

:::question{title="10. Mixing acid" exam="Board level"}
A tank has 400 litres of a 20% acid solution. How many litres of a 5% acid solution must be added so that the mixture is more than 10% but less than 15% acid?

---

Add $x$ litres. Acid $= 0.05x + 80$ litres (20% of 400 is 80); volume $= 400 + x$ litres.

More than 10%: $0.05x + 80 > 0.10(400 + x)$. Times 100: $5x + 8000 > 10x + 4000$, so $x < 800$.

Less than 15%: $0.05x + 80 < 0.15(400 + x)$. Times 100: $5x + 8000 < 15x + 6000$, so $x > 200$.

So **more than 200 litres but less than 800 litres**.
:::
