## Current and potential difference

:::question{title="1. Charge and electrons" exam="Board level"}
A current of 0.4 A flows through a wire for 2 minutes. Find the charge that flows, and the number of electrons in it.

---

$t = 2$ min $= 120$ s. $Q = It = 0.4 \times 120 =$ **48 C**.

$n = \dfrac{Q}{e} = \dfrac{48}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}} =$ **$3 \times 10^{20}$ electrons**.
:::

:::question{title="2. Finding the voltage" exam="Board level"}
360 J of work is done in moving 30 C of charge through a lamp. What is the potential difference across the lamp?

---

$V = \dfrac{W}{Q} = \dfrac{360}{30} =$ **12 V**.
:::

## Ohm's law and resistance

:::question{title="3. Same resistor, new voltage" exam="Board level"}
A resistor takes 0.5 A from a 6 V source. What current will it take from a 9 V source? (Its temperature stays the same.)

---

$R = \dfrac{V}{I} = \dfrac{6}{0.5} = 12\ \Omega$. New current $I = \dfrac{9}{12} =$ **0.75 A**.
:::

:::question{title="4. Copper wire" exam="Board level"}
A copper wire is 50 m long and has an area of cross-section of 0.8 mm². Find its resistance. (Resistivity of copper $= 1.6 \times 10^{-8}\ \Omega$ m.)

---

$A = 0.8$ mm² $= 0.8 \times 10^{-6}$ m² $= 8 \times 10^{-7}$ m².

$\rho l = 1.6 \times 10^{-8} \times 50 = 8 \times 10^{-7}\ \Omega$ m².

$R = \dfrac{\rho l}{A} = \dfrac{8 \times 10^{-7}}{8 \times 10^{-7}} =$ **1 Ω**.
:::

:::question{title="5. Longer and thicker" exam="Board level"}
A wire has a resistance of 8 Ω. A second wire of the same material is twice as long and has twice the diameter. Find its resistance.

---

Twice the diameter means 4 times the area. $R_2 = \rho \dfrac{2l}{4A} = \dfrac{1}{2} \times \rho\dfrac{l}{A} = \dfrac{8}{2} =$ **4 Ω**.
:::

## Series and parallel

:::question{title="6. Series circuit" exam="Board level"}
Resistors of 2 Ω, 4 Ω and 6 Ω are joined in series to a 6 V battery. Find the current, and the potential difference across the 4 Ω resistor.

---

$R_s = 2 + 4 + 6 = 12\ \Omega$. $I = \dfrac{6}{12} =$ **0.5 A** (same in all three).

$V_{4\Omega} = IR = 0.5 \times 4 =$ **2 V**.
:::

:::question{title="7. Parallel circuit" exam="Board level"}
Resistors of 3 Ω and 6 Ω are joined in parallel across a 6 V battery. Find the total resistance, the total current, and the current through the 6 Ω resistor.

---

$R_p = \dfrac{3 \times 6}{3 + 6} =$ **2 Ω**. Total current $I = \dfrac{6}{2} =$ **3 A**.

Each resistor has the full 6 V across it, so $I_{6\Omega} = \dfrac{6}{6} =$ **1 A** (and 2 A goes through the 3 Ω).
:::

:::question{title="8. Making 6 Ω" exam="Board level"}
You have three resistors of 4 Ω each. How will you join all three to get 6 Ω? What current will this combination draw from a 12 V battery?

---

Join two of them in parallel: $\dfrac{4}{2} = 2\ \Omega$. Put the third in series with this pair: $2 + 4 = 6\ \Omega$.

So: **two in parallel, then the third in series with them**. Current $= \dfrac{12}{6} =$ **2 A**.
:::

## Heating, power and energy

:::question{title="9. Heater and fuse" exam="Board level"}
A heater is rated 880 W, 220 V. Find (a) the current it draws, (b) its resistance, (c) the heat it produces in 1 minute. (d) Which fuse should be used: 1 A, 2 A, 3 A, 5 A or 10 A?

---

(a) $I = \dfrac{P}{V} = \dfrac{880}{220} =$ **4 A**.

(b) $R = \dfrac{V}{I} = \dfrac{220}{4} =$ **55 Ω**.

(c) $H = Pt = 880 \times 60 =$ **52 800 J** (52.8 kJ).

(d) The next rating above 4 A: a **5 A** fuse.
:::

:::question{title="10. Electricity bill" exam="Board level"}
A house uses a 2 kW geyser for 1 hour a day and four 60 W bulbs for 5 hours a day. Find the cost for 30 days at ₹5 per kW h.

---

Geyser: $2 \times 1 = 2$ kW h per day. Bulbs: $4 \times 60 \times 5 = 1200$ W h $= 1.2$ kW h per day.

Total per day $= 3.2$ kW h. For 30 days: $3.2 \times 30 = 96$ kW h.

Cost $= 96 \times 5 =$ **₹480**.
:::
