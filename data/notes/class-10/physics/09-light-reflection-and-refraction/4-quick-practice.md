## Mirrors

:::question{title="1. Concave mirror, far object" exam="Board level"}
A 3 cm tall object is placed 30 cm in front of a concave mirror of focal length 10 cm. Find the position, size and nature of the image.

---

$u = -30$ cm, $f = -10$ cm.

$\frac1v = \frac1f - \frac1u = -\frac{1}{10} + \frac{1}{30} = -\frac{2}{30}$, so $v = -15$ cm.

$m = -\frac{v}{u} = -\frac{-15}{-30} = -0.5$, so $h' = -0.5 \times 3 = -1.5$ cm.

**Image 15 cm in front of the mirror, 1.5 cm tall, real, inverted and diminished.**
:::

:::question{title="2. Shaving mirror" exam="Board level"}
A concave mirror has a radius of curvature of 24 cm. A face is 8 cm from it. Where is the image, and how much is it magnified?

---

$f = \frac{R}{2} = -12$ cm, $u = -8$ cm.

$\frac1v = -\frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{8} = \frac{1}{24}$, so $v = +24$ cm.

$m = -\frac{v}{u} = -\frac{24}{-8} = +3$.

**Image 24 cm behind the mirror, virtual, erect and 3 times enlarged.**
:::

:::question{title="3. Rear-view mirror" exam="Board level"}
A 6 cm tall object is 20 cm in front of a convex mirror of radius of curvature 40 cm. Find the image.

---

$f = +\frac{40}{2} = +20$ cm, $u = -20$ cm.

$\frac1v = \frac{1}{20} + \frac{1}{20} = \frac{1}{10}$, so $v = +10$ cm.

$m = -\frac{10}{-20} = +0.5$, so $h' = +3$ cm.

**Image 10 cm behind the mirror, 3 cm tall, virtual, erect and diminished.**
:::

:::question{title="4. Find the focal length" exam="Board level"}
A concave mirror forms a real image 4 times the size of the object. The object is 15 cm from the mirror. Find the image distance and the focal length.

---

A real image has negative $m$, so $m = -4$.

$-\frac{v}{u} = -4$ gives $v = 4u = 4 \times (-15) = -60$ cm.

$\frac1f = \frac1v + \frac1u = -\frac{1}{60} - \frac{4}{60} = -\frac{5}{60}$, so $f = -12$ cm.

**Image 60 cm in front of the mirror; focal length 12 cm ($f = -12$ cm).**
:::

## Refraction

:::question{title="5. Speeds and refractive index" exam="Board level"}
Light travels at $2.0 \times 10^8$ m/s in glass and $2.25 \times 10^8$ m/s in water. Take $c = 3.0 \times 10^8$ m/s.

(a) Find the refractive index of glass with respect to water.

(b) Find the absolute refractive index of water.

---

(a) $n_{gw} = \dfrac{v_{water}}{v_{glass}} = \dfrac{2.25}{2.0} =$ **1.125**.

(b) $n_w = \dfrac{c}{v_{water}} = \dfrac{3.0}{2.25} \approx$ **1.33**.
:::

:::question{title="6. Water into kerosene" exam="Board level"}
Refractive indices: water 1.33, kerosene 1.44. A ray goes obliquely from water into kerosene. Does it bend towards or away from the normal? In which liquid is light faster?

---

Kerosene has the larger refractive index, so it is optically denser (even though it is lighter than water). Going from rarer to denser, the ray bends **towards the normal**. Light is **faster in water**.
:::

## Lenses and power

:::question{title="7. Magnifying glass" exam="Board level"}
An object is 10 cm from a convex lens of focal length 15 cm. Find the position and nature of the image.

---

$u = -10$ cm, $f = +15$ cm.

$\frac1v = \frac1f + \frac1u = \frac{1}{15} - \frac{1}{10} = -\frac{1}{30}$, so $v = -30$ cm.

$m = \frac{v}{u} = \frac{-30}{-10} = +3$.

**Image 30 cm from the lens on the same side as the object; virtual, erect, 3 times enlarged.**
:::

:::question{title="8. Concave lens" exam="Board level"}
A 4 cm tall object is 20 cm from a concave lens of focal length 20 cm. Find the image position and height.

---

$u = -20$ cm, $f = -20$ cm.

$\frac1v = \frac1f + \frac1u = -\frac{1}{20} - \frac{1}{20} = -\frac{1}{10}$, so $v = -10$ cm.

$m = \frac{v}{u} = \frac{-10}{-20} = +0.5$, so $h' = +2$ cm.

**Image 10 cm from the lens on the object's side, 2 cm tall, virtual and erect.**
:::

:::question{title="9. Image on a screen" exam="Board level"}
A convex lens forms a real image, twice the size of the object, on a screen 60 cm from the lens. Find the object distance, the focal length and the power of the lens.

---

Real image: $m = -2$ and $v = +60$ cm.

$m = \frac{v}{u}$ gives $u = \frac{60}{-2} = -30$ cm.

$\frac1f = \frac1v - \frac1u = \frac{1}{60} + \frac{1}{30} = \frac{3}{60}$, so $f = +20$ cm $= 0.20$ m.

$P = \frac{1}{0.20} = +5$ D.

**Object 30 cm from the lens; $f = 20$ cm; $P = +5$ D.**
:::

:::question{title="10. Two lenses in contact" exam="Board level"}
A convex lens of focal length 25 cm is placed in contact with a concave lens of focal length 50 cm. Find the power of each lens and of the combination. Is the combination converging or diverging?

---

$P_1 = \frac{1}{+0.25} = +4$ D.

$P_2 = \frac{1}{-0.50} = -2$ D.

$P = P_1 + P_2 = +2$ D, so $f = \frac{1}{2} = 0.5$ m.

**+4 D, −2 D and +2 D; the combination is converging (like a convex lens of focal length 50 cm).**
:::
