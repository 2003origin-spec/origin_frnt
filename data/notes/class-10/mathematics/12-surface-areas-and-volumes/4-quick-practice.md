## Surface area

:::question{title="1. Two cubes" exam="Board level"}
Two cubes, each of edge 5 cm, are joined end to end. Find the surface area of the cuboid formed. (No $\pi$ needed.)

---

Two faces are stuck together, so $10$ faces show: $10 \times 5^2 =$ **250 cm²**.
:::

:::question{title="2. Toy" exam="Board level"}
A toy is a cone on a hemisphere, both of radius 7 cm. The height of the cone is 24 cm. Find the surface area of the toy. (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.)

---

$l = \sqrt{7^2 + 24^2} = 25$ cm.

SA $= \pi r l + 2\pi r^2$

$= \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25 + 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 49$

$= 550 + 308 =$ **858 cm²**.
:::

:::question{title="3. Tent" exam="Board level"}
A tent is a cylinder of diameter 14 m and height 3 m with a conical top of slant height 10 m. The floor is not covered. Find the canvas needed and its cost at ₹50 per m². (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.)

---

Canvas $= 2\pi r h + \pi r l$

$= 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 3 + \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 10$

$= 132 + 220 = 352$ m².

Cost $= 352 \times 50 =$ **352 m², ₹17,600**.
:::

:::question{title="4. Bowl in a cube" exam="Board level"}
A hemisphere of diameter 14 cm is scooped out of one face of a cube of edge 14 cm. Find the surface area of the remaining solid. (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.)

---

$r = 7$. SA $= 6a^2 - \pi r^2 + 2\pi r^2 = 6a^2 + \pi r^2$.

$= 6 \times 196 + \frac{22}{7} \times 49 = 1176 + 154 =$ **1330 cm²**.
:::

:::question{title="5. Conical hole" exam="Board level"}
From a solid cylinder of radius 3 cm and height 4 cm, a cone of the same radius and height is hollowed out. Find the total surface area of the remaining solid. (Take $\pi = 3.14$.)

---

Slant height $l = \sqrt{9 + 16} = 5$ cm.

SA $= 2\pi r h + \pi r^2 + \pi r l = \pi(24 + 9 + 15) = 48\pi$.

$= 48 \times 3.14 =$ **150.72 cm²**.
:::

## Volume

:::question{title="6. Tank" exam="Board level"}
A tank is a cylinder with a hemisphere at each end. Its diameter is 6 m and its total length is 16 m. Find its volume. (Take $\pi = 3.14$.)

---

$r = 3$ m. Cylinder length $= 16 - 2 \times 3 = 10$ m.

$V = \pi r^2 h + \frac43\pi r^3 = 90\pi + 36\pi = 126\pi$.

$= 126 \times 3.14 =$ **395.64 m³**.
:::

:::question{title="7. Wooden stand" exam="Board level"}
A wooden block is a cuboid 20 cm × 10 cm × 5 cm. Three conical holes, each of radius 1.4 cm and depth 3 cm, are cut into it. Find the volume of wood left. (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.)

---

One cone $= \frac13 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 1.4^2 \times 3 = 6.16$ cm³.

Wood $= 1000 - 3 \times 6.16 = 1000 - 18.48 =$ **981.52 cm³**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Cylinder around a toy" exam="Board level"}
A toy is a cone of height 4 cm standing on a hemisphere of radius 3 cm. A cylinder just fits around it. Find the difference between the volume of the cylinder and the volume of the toy. (Take $\pi = 3.14$.)

---

Toy $= \frac23\pi(27) + \frac13\pi(9)(4) = 18\pi + 12\pi = 30\pi$.

Cylinder: radius 3, height $4 + 3 = 7$, so $63\pi$.

Difference $= 33\pi = 33 \times 3.14 =$ **103.62 cm³**.
:::

## Harder ones

:::question{title="9. Water left" exam="Board level"}
A cylinder of radius 6 cm and height 15 cm is full of water. A solid made of a cone of height 9 cm on a hemisphere of radius 6 cm is lowered into it until it touches the bottom. Find the volume of water left. Give the answer in terms of $\pi$.

---

Cylinder $= \pi \times 36 \times 15 = 540\pi$.

Solid $= \frac23\pi(216) + \frac13\pi(36)(9) = 144\pi + 108\pi = 252\pi$. Its height is $6 + 9 = 15$ cm, so it goes fully under water.

Water left $= 540\pi - 252\pi =$ **288π cm³**.
:::

:::question{title="10. Steel balls" exam="Board level"}
An inverted cone of radius 6 cm and height 12 cm is full of water. Steel balls of radius 1 cm are dropped in, until one-third of the water has flowed out. How many balls were dropped? (No value of $\pi$ needed: it cancels.)

---

Cone $= \frac13\pi(36)(12) = 144\pi$. Water out $= \frac13 \times 144\pi = 48\pi$.

One ball $= \frac43\pi(1)^3 = \frac43\pi$.

Number $= 48\pi \div \frac43\pi =$ **36 balls**.
:::
