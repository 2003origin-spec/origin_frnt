## NCERT one-liners

:::remember
- A **sector** is enclosed by two radii and the arc between them.
- A **segment** is enclosed by a chord and the arc on one side of it.
- The angle of the major sector is $360^\circ - \angle AOB$.
- "Sector" and "segment" mean the **minor** ones unless stated otherwise.
- Area of a segment = area of the sector − area of the triangle.
:::

## Sector or segment?

:::trap
Read the region carefully. **Two radii** → sector. **A chord** → segment. A segment needs the triangle subtracted; a sector does not.
:::

:::trap
**Major segment is not "major sector − triangle".** The triangle $OAB$ lies inside the major segment, so

major segment = major sector **+** triangle = $\pi r^2 -$ minor segment.
:::

- A semicircle is a sector of $180^\circ$ and also a segment (its chord is a diameter). Its "triangle" has zero area.

## The triangle OAB

:::trap
$\dfrac12 r^2$ is the triangle's area **only when** $\theta = 90^\circ$. For $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ it is $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}r^2$. For other angles, draw $OM \perp AB$ and use $\dfrac{\theta}{2}$.
:::

:::trap
In $\triangle OMA$, the angle at $O$ is $\dfrac{\theta}{2}$, **not** $\theta$. And the chord is $AB = 2\,AM$, not $AM$.
:::

:::tip
$60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$ give the **same** triangle area, $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}r^2$. (The $60^\circ$ triangle is tall and narrow; the $120^\circ$ one is short and wide.)
:::

## Numbers and units

:::trap
Use the $\pi$ the question gives. The exercise default is $\dfrac{22}{7}$, but many questions say $3.14$. Use the given $\sqrt{3}$ too ($1.73$ or $1.7$). Mixing values gives a slightly wrong answer.
:::

:::trap
Arc length is in **cm**; area is in **cm²**. Do not mix up $\dfrac{\theta}{360} \times 2\pi r$ (length) with $\dfrac{\theta}{360} \times \pi r^2$ (area).
:::

- $\theta$ must be in **degrees**. Class 10 does not use radians.
- If you get a circumference or a diameter, find $r$ first. A circumference of 22 cm gives $r = \dfrac{7}{2}$ cm.
- Keep $\sqrt{3}$ in the answer until the last line, unless a decimal is asked.

## Word problems

:::trap
**Clock hands:** the minute hand moves $6^\circ$ per minute, so 5 minutes is $30^\circ$, not $5^\circ$. The hour hand moves only $30^\circ$ per hour.
:::

:::trap
**Two wipers** (not overlapping): multiply one sector by **2**. **Ribs of an umbrella:** 8 ribs make 8 sectors of $45^\circ$ each.
:::

:::trap
**Increase in grazing area** when the rope gets longer: subtract the two quadrants. Do not find the quadrant of the difference in length. $\dfrac14\pi(10^2 - 5^2)$, not $\dfrac14\pi(5^2)$.
:::

- **Wire for a brooch** = circumference + all the diameters inside it. 5 diameters of length $d$ add $5d$.
- **Cost of designs** = (total area) × (rate per cm²).

## Shortcuts

:::tip
**Sector as a fraction:** $\dfrac{\theta}{360}$ of the circle. $30^\circ \to \dfrac{1}{12}$, $45^\circ \to \dfrac18$, $60^\circ \to \dfrac16$, $90^\circ \to \dfrac14$, $120^\circ \to \dfrac13$.
:::

:::tip
**Area from the arc** (beyond the Class 10 book): sector area $= \dfrac12 \times l \times r$, just like a triangle with base $l$ and height $r$. A quick check on your answer.
:::

:::tip
**Quadrant segment** ($\theta = 90^\circ$) $= \left(\dfrac{\pi}{4} - \dfrac12\right)r^2$. With $\pi = 3.14$ this is $0.285\,r^2$.
:::
