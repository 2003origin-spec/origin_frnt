## Variation

:::question{title="1. Older trait" exam="Board level"}
In a population of bacteria that reproduce asexually, trait P is found in 5% of the bacteria and trait Q in 45%. Which trait most likely arose earlier?

---

**Trait Q.** A trait that has been around for more generations has had more time to be copied into more individuals.
:::

:::question{title="2. More variation" exam="Board level"}
Why do offspring from sexual reproduction show more variation than offspring from asexual reproduction?

---

In asexual reproduction, the only differences come from small errors in DNA copying. In sexual reproduction, **DNA from two different parents is combined**, as well as these copying errors, so many new combinations appear.
:::

## One trait

:::question{title="3. Counting the F2" exam="Board level"}
Tall F1 pea plants (Tt) are self-pollinated and give 800 plants. About how many are short, and how many are TT?

---

The F2 is 1 TT : 2 Tt : 1 tt. Short (tt) $= \tfrac14 \times 800$. TT $= \tfrac14 \times 800$. So about **200 short** and **200 TT**.
:::

:::question{title="4. All tall offspring" exam="Board level"}
A tall pea plant is crossed with a short plant. All 60 offspring are tall. What is the tall parent's genetic make-up?

---

**TT.** If it were Tt, about half the offspring (Tt × tt gives 1 tall : 1 short) would have been short.
:::

:::question{title="5. Only tall offspring" exam="Board level"}
An F2 from a tall × short cross has 300 tall plants. Each tall plant is self-pollinated on its own. About how many of them give **only** tall offspring?

---

The tall plants are 1 TT : 2 Tt. Only TT gives only tall offspring: $\tfrac13 \times 300 =$ **100**. The other 200 (Tt) give tall and short plants.
:::

## Two traits

:::question{title="6. Wrinkled green seeds" exam="Board level"}
Pea plants with round, green seeds (RRyy) are crossed with plants with wrinkled, yellow seeds (rrYY). The F1 plants are self-pollinated and give 1600 seeds. About how many seeds are wrinkled and green, and is this a parental type or a new combination?

---

Wrinkled green is 1 part in 9 : 3 : 3 : 1, so $\tfrac{1}{16} \times 1600 = 100$. Neither parent was wrinkled green, so it is a new combination: **100 seeds, a new combination**.
:::

:::question{title="7. Violet flowers" exam="Board level"}
Tall plants with violet flowers (TTWW) are crossed with short plants with white flowers (ttww). What do all the F1 plants look like, and which kinds of germ cells do they make?

---

All F1 plants are TtWw: **tall with violet flowers**. They make **4 kinds** of germ cells in equal numbers: **TW, Tw, tW, tw**.
:::

## Genes and sex

:::question{title="8. Short plant" exam="Board level"}
A pea plant has an altered gene for an enzyme that helps make a growth hormone. The enzyme now works less efficiently. How will this affect the plant, and why?

---

The plant will be **short**. The less efficient enzyme makes less growth hormone, so the plant grows less. The gene controls the trait through the protein (enzyme) it codes for.
:::

:::question{title="9. Fourth child" exam="Board level"}
A couple already has three sons. What is the chance that their fourth child will be a girl?

---

**1 in 2 (50%).** Each child gets an X from the mother. Whether the father gives an X or a Y is 50 : 50 each time, whatever happened before.
:::

:::question{title="10. Human sperm" exam="Board level"}
How many chromosomes are there in a human sperm, and what are the two kinds of sperm?

---

**23 chromosomes.** One kind carries **22 + X**, the other **22 + Y**. An X sperm gives a girl (XX) and a Y sperm gives a boy (XY).
:::
