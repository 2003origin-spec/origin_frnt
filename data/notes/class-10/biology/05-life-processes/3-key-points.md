## Nutrition

:::trap
**Fungi are heterotrophs**, not autotrophs. Yeast, bread mould and mushrooms break food down **outside** the body and then absorb it. Autotrophs are green plants and **some bacteria**.
:::

- Photosynthesis needs **all four**: carbon dioxide, water, sunlight and chlorophyll.
- In the NCERT equation, **12** water molecules go in and **6** come out. Do not cancel them when you write the NCERT form.
- Stomata **open** when guard cells **swell** with water, and **close** when they shrink.
- Desert plants take in $\ce{CO2}$ at **night** and use light on it in the **day**. So the steps of photosynthesis need not happen together.

:::trap
In the variegated-leaf test, the leaf is boiled in **alcohol** (on a water-bath) to remove the **chlorophyll**, not to test for starch. **Iodine** is the starch test. The plant is first kept in the dark for 3 days to use up the starch already there.
:::

## Digestion

:::remember
- **Saliva:** starch → simple sugar (salivary amylase).
- **Stomach:** protein digestion starts (pepsin, in an acidic medium).
- **Small intestine:** digestion of carbohydrates, proteins and fats is **completed**, and digested food is **absorbed** (villi).
- **Large intestine:** absorbs **water**.
:::

:::trap
**Bile has no enzyme.** It makes food alkaline and **emulsifies** fats (breaks big globules into small ones). The enzyme that digests fat is **lipase**, from the pancreas.
:::

- **Two named enzymes digest protein** in this chapter: **pepsin** (stomach) and **trypsin** (pancreas). Don't swap their places. Intestinal juice then finishes the job (proteins → amino acids).
- HCl does not digest food. It makes the medium **acidic** so pepsin can work. **Mucus** protects the stomach wall from the acid.

## Respiration

:::trap
The first step, glucose → pyruvate, happens in the **cytoplasm** in every organism, with or without oxygen. Only the **aerobic** breakdown of pyruvate happens in the **mitochondria**.
:::

| Pathway | Product made from pyruvate |
| --- | --- |
| Yeast (no oxygen) | Ethanol + $\ce{CO2}$ |
| Muscle (lack of oxygen) | Lactic acid (no $\ce{CO2}$) |
| Aerobic (mitochondria) | $\ce{CO2}$ + water |

:::trap
Muscles short of oxygen make **lactic acid**, not alcohol. And they do **not** give out $\ce{CO2}$ in this step. Lactic acid build-up causes **cramps**.
:::

- Aerobic respiration releases **far more** energy than anaerobic.
- **ATP** is the energy currency. Breaking its last phosphate bond with water releases **30.5 kJ/mol**.
- Plants respire **all the time**. In the day they release no $\ce{CO2}$, because photosynthesis uses it all up.
- Aquatic animals breathe **faster** than land animals, because water has **less dissolved oxygen** than air.

:::trap
Haemoglobin carries **oxygen**. **Carbon dioxide** travels mostly **dissolved** in the blood, because it is more soluble in water than oxygen.
:::

## Heart and blood

:::trap
Arteries carry blood **away** from the heart; veins carry it **towards** the heart. It is **not** "arteries carry oxygen-rich blood". The **pulmonary artery** carries **de-oxygenated** blood (to the lungs), and the **pulmonary veins** carry **oxygen-rich** blood (from the lungs).
:::

- The **left** side of the heart handles **oxygen-rich** blood; the **right** side handles **de-oxygenated** blood.
- **Ventricles** have thicker walls than atria. The **left ventricle** pumps blood to the whole body.
- **Veins** have valves; arteries have thick, elastic walls. **Capillaries** are one cell thick.
- **Fish:** 2 chambers, blood passes the heart **once** per round. **Amphibians and many reptiles:** 3 chambers, some mixing. **Birds and mammals:** 4 chambers, no mixing.
- Normal blood pressure: **120/80 mm Hg**. Systolic = ventricle **contracting**; diastolic = ventricle **relaxing**.

:::trap
**Lymph** is **colourless** and has **less protein** than plasma. It carries **fat** absorbed from the intestine, not oxygen.
:::

## Transport in plants

:::remember
- **Xylem** = water and minerals, from roots **up** to leaves, mostly by physical forces.
- **Phloem** = food, **up and down**, using energy (**ATP**).
:::

- **Translocation** means transport of food in **phloem**, not water in xylem.
- **Root pressure** is more important at **night**; **transpiration pull** is the main force in the **day**.
- Transpiration also helps **cool** the plant (temperature regulation).

## Excretion

:::trap
The unit that **filters** blood is the **nephron**. The **glomerulus** is only the capillary cluster inside it. Don't call the kidney itself the filtering unit.
:::

- Path of urine: kidney → **ureter** → bladder → **urethra**. Ureters are **two**; the urethra is **one**.
- Reabsorbed in the tubule: **glucose, amino acids, salts and most of the water**.
- About **180 L** of filtrate is made a day, but only **1–2 L** of urine is passed.
- An artificial kidney (dialysis) filters but does **no reabsorption**.
- Plants use very different ways from animals. They store wastes in vacuoles, falling leaves, and resins and gums (old xylem), or excrete them into the soil.

## Numbers and names

:::remember
- Alveolar surface: about **80 m²**.
- Oxygen by diffusion alone, lungs to toes: about **3 years**.
- Pyruvate: **3** carbons. Glucose: **6**. Ethanol: **2**. Lactic acid: **3**.
- Blood pressure is measured with a **sphygmomanometer**.
:::
