## Photosynthesis

:::formula{title="Photosynthesis (NCERT equation)"}
$$\begin{aligned} & \ce{6CO2 + 12H2O} \\ & \ce{->[\text{Chlorophyll}][\text{Sunlight}] C6H12O6} \\ & \ce{+ 6O2 + 6H2O} \end{aligned}$$
$\ce{C6H12O6}$ = glucose. Needs: carbon dioxide, water, sunlight and chlorophyll.

Extra glucose is stored as **starch** in plants. Animals store extra energy as **glycogen**.
:::

:::formula{title="Three events in photosynthesis"}
1. Chlorophyll **absorbs light** energy.
2. Light energy becomes **chemical energy**, and **water is split** into hydrogen and oxygen.
3. Carbon dioxide is **reduced** to carbohydrates.

The steps need not happen one right after the other. Desert plants take in $\ce{CO2}$ at **night** and use light energy on it in the **day**.
:::

## Digestion in humans

| Gland or organ | Juice | What is in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salivary glands | Saliva | Salivary amylase |
| Gastric glands (stomach wall) | Gastric juice | HCl, pepsin, mucus |
| Liver | Bile | Bile salts |
| Pancreas | Pancreatic juice | Trypsin, lipase |
| Small intestine wall | Intestinal juice | Enzymes for the last step |

:::formula{title="What each one does"}
- **Salivary amylase:** starch → simple sugar.
- **HCl:** makes the stomach acidic, so pepsin can work.
- **Pepsin:** digests proteins.
- **Mucus:** protects the stomach lining from the acid.
- **Bile:** makes the food from the stomach **alkaline**. Bile salts break big fat globules into small ones (**emulsification**).
- **Trypsin:** digests proteins. **Lipase:** breaks down emulsified fats.
- **Intestinal juice:** proteins → amino acids, complex carbohydrates → glucose, fats → fatty acids + glycerol.
:::

## Respiration

:::formula{title="Step 1 (in every organism)"}
Glucose (6-carbon) → pyruvate (3-carbon) + energy

This happens in the **cytoplasm**.
:::

| Condition | Case | Products from pyruvate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No oxygen | Yeast | Ethanol + $\ce{CO2}$ + energy |
| Lack of oxygen | Our muscle cells | Lactic acid + energy |
| Oxygen present | Mitochondria | $\ce{CO2}$ + water + energy |

- Ethanol is a **2-carbon** molecule. Lactic acid is a **3-carbon** molecule.
- The oxygen pathway (aerobic) releases **much more** energy.

:::formula{title="ATP: the energy currency"}
ADP + phosphate + energy → ATP

When water breaks the last phosphate bond of ATP, **30.5 kJ/mol** of energy is released.
:::

## Heart and blood

:::formula{title="Path of blood through the heart"}
Body → vena cava → right atrium → right ventricle → pulmonary artery → **lungs**

Lungs → pulmonary veins → left atrium → left ventricle → aorta → **body**

Blood passes through the heart **twice** in one round of the body: **double circulation**.
:::

| Animal | Chambers | Circulation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fish | 2 | Single (heart once per round) |
| Amphibians, many reptiles | 3 | Double, some mixing |
| Birds, mammals | 4 | Double, no mixing |

| Vessel | Job | Wall |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Artery | Away from the heart | Thick, elastic |
| Vein | Back to the heart | Thinner, has valves |
| Capillary | Exchange with cells | One cell thick |

:::formula{title="Blood, lymph and what they carry"}
- **Plasma:** food, $\ce{CO2}$ and nitrogen wastes, dissolved.
- **Red blood corpuscles:** oxygen (with haemoglobin).
- **Platelets:** help blood clot at a cut.
- **Lymph:** colourless, less protein than plasma. Carries absorbed **fat** from the intestine and returns extra tissue fluid to the blood.
:::

:::formula{title="Blood pressure"}
- Normal: systolic **120 mm Hg** (ventricle contracts), diastolic **80 mm Hg** (ventricle relaxes).
- Measured with a **sphygmomanometer**.
- High blood pressure = **hypertension**.
:::

## Transport in plants

| Feature | Xylem | Phloem |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Carries | Water and minerals | Food (e.g. sucrose), amino acids |
| Direction | Upwards, root to leaf | Up and down |
| Cells | Vessels, tracheids | Sieve tubes, companion cells |
| Energy | Mostly physical forces | Uses ATP |

## Excretion

:::formula{title="Human excretory system"}
Kidneys → ureters → urinary bladder → urethra

- Filtering unit: the **nephron**. Its parts: **glomerulus** (a cluster of thin capillaries) + **Bowman's capsule** (a cup) + a coiled **tubule**.
- Initial filtrate: about **180 L** a day. Urine passed: only **1–2 L** a day. The rest is reabsorbed.
:::
