## Nervous system

:::question{title="1. Chemical signal" exam="Board level"}
In which part of a neuron is the electrical impulse changed into a chemical signal?

(a) Dendrite tip (b) Cell body (c) End of the axon (d) Nucleus

---

At the end of the axon, the impulse makes the neuron release chemicals that cross the synapse. **(c) End of the axon.**
:::

:::question{title="2. Order of the reflex arc" exam="Board level"}
Put these in the correct order for a reflex arc: motor neuron, receptor, relay neuron, effector, sensory neuron.

---

The receptor picks up the stimulus, the sensory neuron carries it to the spinal cord, the relay neuron passes it on, and the motor neuron carries the order to the muscle. **Receptor → sensory neuron → relay neuron → motor neuron → effector.**
:::

:::question{title="3. Riding a bicycle" exam="Board level"}
A person can no longer walk in a straight line or keep balance on a bicycle, but can think and speak normally. Which part of the brain is most likely affected?

---

Precision of voluntary actions, posture and balance are controlled by the **cerebellum** (in the hind-brain).
:::

:::question{title="4. Odd one out" exam="Board level"}
Which one of these is **not** controlled by the medulla: blood pressure, salivation, vomiting, feeling full after a meal?

---

The medulla controls blood pressure, salivation and vomiting. The feeling of being full comes from the hunger centre in the fore-brain. **Feeling full after a meal.**
:::

:::question{title="5. Reflex or not" exam="Board level"}
Which of these is a reflex action?

(a) Writing a letter (b) Pulling the hand away from a hot pan (c) Moving a chair (d) Clapping at the end of a show

---

(a), (c) and (d) are voluntary: we decide to do them. Pulling the hand from a hot pan is sudden and needs no thinking. **(b).**
:::

## Plants

:::question{title="6. Sensitive plant" exam="Board level"}
How do the leaves of the sensitive plant (chhui-mui) fold when touched, though the plant has no muscles?

---

The message of touch passes from cell to cell by electrical-chemical means. Cells then **change the amount of water in them**, so they swell or shrink and change shape. No growth is involved.
:::

:::question{title="7. Bending to light" exam="Board level"}
A potted plant is kept near a window, with light coming from the left side only. On which side of the shoot does auxin collect, and which way does the shoot bend?

---

Auxin diffuses to the shady side, which here is the **right side**. Cells there grow longer, so the shoot **bends to the left, towards the light**.
:::

:::question{title="8. Match the hormone" exam="Board level"}
Which plant hormone (a) promotes cell division and is plentiful in fruits and seeds, and (b) inhibits growth and causes wilting of leaves?

---

(a) **Cytokinins.** (b) **Abscisic acid.**
:::

## Animal hormones

:::question{title="9. Swollen neck" exam="Board level"}
People in a region eat food with very little iodine. Many of them get a swollen neck. Name the disease, the gland involved and the hormone that is short.

---

Iodine is needed to make thyroxin. Too little iodine means too little thyroxin, and the thyroid in the neck swells. **Goitre; thyroid gland; thyroxin.** Iodised salt helps prevent it.
:::

:::question{title="10. Feedback" exam="Board level"}
A student eats a sweet meal and her blood sugar rises. Which gland responds, what does it do, and what happens when the sugar level falls again?

---

Cells of the **pancreas** detect the rise and release **more insulin**, which brings blood sugar down. As the sugar level falls, **insulin release is reduced**. This is a **feedback mechanism**.
:::
