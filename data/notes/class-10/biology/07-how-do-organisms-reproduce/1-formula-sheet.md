## The basic idea

:::formula{title="Reproduction starts with a DNA copy"}
A cell makes a copy of its DNA and also makes extra cell apparatus. Then the cell divides into two.

DNA copying is **not absolutely accurate**. So the copies are similar, but may not be identical. These small changes are **variations**.
:::

:::formula{title="Chromosome number in sexual reproduction"}
Germ-cells have **half** the number of chromosomes and half the DNA of body cells. They are made by **meiosis**.

Two germ-cells fuse, so the zygote gets the **full number back**.
:::

## Asexual reproduction (one parent)

| Mode | What happens | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Binary fission | Cell splits into two | Amoeba, Leishmania, many bacteria |
| Multiple fission | Cell splits into many at once | Plasmodium |
| Fragmentation | Body breaks into pieces; each grows | Spirogyra |
| Regeneration | A cut piece grows into a full organism | Hydra, Planaria |
| Budding | An outgrowth (bud) grows and detaches | Hydra, yeast |
| Vegetative propagation | Root, stem or leaf makes a new plant | Potato, Bryophyllum, money-plant |
| Spore formation | Sporangia (blobs) hold spores | Rhizopus (bread mould) |

:::formula{title="Binary fission: the plane of division"}
- **Amoeba:** splits in **any plane**.
- **Leishmania** (causes kala-azar): splits in a **definite orientation**, set by the whip-like structure at one end of the cell.
:::

:::formula{title="Vegetative propagation: uses"}
- Methods: **layering** and **grafting** (sugarcane, roses, grapes).
- Plants flower and fruit **earlier** than plants grown from seed.
- Grows plants that have **lost the capacity to make seeds**: banana, orange, rose, jasmine.
- New plants are genetically similar enough to the parent to have all its characteristics.
:::

:::formula{title="Tissue culture (steps)"}
1. Take tissue or cells from the **growing tip** of a plant.
2. Put them in an artificial medium. They divide to form a **callus**.
3. Move the callus to a medium with **hormones** for growth and differentiation.
4. Plant the **plantlets** in soil.

Gives many plants from one parent, in disease-free conditions. Common for ornamental plants.
:::

## Flower parts

| Part | Male or female | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sepals, petals | Neither | Not reproductive parts |
| Stamen (anther) | Male | Makes pollen grains |
| Pistil | Female | Has the ovary with ovules |

:::formula{title="Parts of the pistil (bottom to top)"}
- **Ovary:** swollen bottom part. Has ovules; each ovule has an **egg cell**.
- **Style:** middle, long part.
- **Stigma:** top part; may be sticky.
:::

| Flower type | Has | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Unisexual | Stamens **or** pistil | Papaya, watermelon |
| Bisexual | Stamens **and** pistil | Hibiscus, mustard |

## After fertilisation in a flower

| This part | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| Zygote | Embryo |
| Ovule | Seed (with a tough coat) |
| Ovary | Fruit |
| Petals, sepals, stamens, style, stigma | May shrivel and fall off |

## Human male reproductive system

| Part | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Testes | Make sperms; secrete **testosterone** |
| Scrotum | Holds the testes outside the abdomen (cooler) |
| Vas deferens | Carries sperms |
| Seminal vesicles, prostate | Add fluid: easier transport and nutrition |
| Urethra | Common passage for sperms and urine |
| Penis | Organ for transfer of sperms |

## Human female reproductive system

| Part | Job |
| --- | --- |
| Ovaries | Make eggs; make some hormones |
| Oviduct (fallopian tube) | Carries the egg; **fertilisation** happens here |
| Uterus (womb) | Embryo implants and grows here |
| Cervix | Opening of the uterus into the vagina |
| Vagina | Sperms enter here during intercourse |

:::formula{title="Numbers to remember"}
- From puberty, one egg is released **every month** by one of the ovaries.
- An unfertilised egg lives for **about one day**.
- Menstruation comes roughly **every month** and lasts about **2 to 8 days**.
- A baby develops inside the mother for about **nine months**.
:::

## Contraception

| Type | Example | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barrier | Condom; vaginal coverings | Sperm cannot reach the egg |
| Hormonal | Oral pills | Eggs are not released |
| Device in uterus | Loop, copper-T | Prevents pregnancy |
| Surgical | Vas deferens or fallopian tube blocked | Sperm or egg cannot pass |

Of these, NCERT names only the **condom** as also helping (to some extent) against sexually transmitted diseases.

## Sexually transmitted diseases

| Caused by | Examples |
