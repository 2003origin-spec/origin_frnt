## DNA copying and variation

:::remember
- Reproduction is **not essential** to keep an individual alive.
- The basic event in reproduction is the **creation of a DNA copy**, along with extra cell apparatus.
- DNA copying is **not absolutely accurate**. This makes variation, the basis for evolution.
:::

:::trap
Variation helps the **species** survive. It does **not** always help the **individual**. A big change in a niche can kill most individuals; only a few variants survive.
:::

- Reproduction gives **stability** to populations, because DNA copying is consistent enough to keep the body design that suits the niche.

## Asexual reproduction

:::trap
Don't mix up the organisms:
- **Amoeba:** binary fission, in **any plane**.
- **Leishmania:** binary fission, in a **definite orientation**.
- **Plasmodium:** **multiple** fission.
- **Yeast:** **budding** (not fission).
- **Hydra:** budding **and** regeneration.
- **Planaria:** regeneration.
- **Spirogyra:** fragmentation.
- **Rhizopus:** spore formation.
:::

- **Kala-azar** is caused by **Leishmania**. **Malaria** is caused by **Plasmodium**.
- In Rhizopus, the **hyphae** are **not** reproductive. The **sporangia** (the blobs) hold the spores.
- Spores have **thick walls**, so they survive until they reach a moist surface.

:::trap
**Regeneration is not reproduction.** Most organisms do not normally depend on being cut up to reproduce. Complex organisms cannot regenerate a whole body, because their cells form tissues and organs at fixed places.
:::

- **Bryophyllum** makes buds on the **leaf margin**. **Potato** makes buds in the **notches** (eyes) on its surface.
- A potato piece **without** a bud does not grow.

:::remember
Plants that have **lost the capacity to make seeds**, so they are grown by vegetative propagation: **banana, orange, rose, jasmine**.
Grown by layering or grafting: **sugarcane, roses, grapes**.
:::

- Tissue culture starts from cells at the **growing tip**. The mass of cells formed is the **callus**. It is used mostly for **ornamental plants**.

## Sexual reproduction

- Sexual reproduction makes **more variation** than asexual reproduction, because it combines DNA from two individuals.
- Germ-cells have **half** the chromosomes. They are made by **meiosis**. Fusion restores the full number.
- The **motile** gamete is called **male**. The gamete with **stored food** is called **female**.

## Flowers

:::trap
**Pollination is not fertilisation.**
- **Pollination:** pollen moves from **anther to stigma**.
- **Fertilisation:** the male germ-cell **fuses** with the egg in the ovule.

Pollination comes first.
:::

- **Unisexual:** papaya, watermelon. **Bisexual:** Hibiscus, mustard.
- The **anther** (part of the stamen) contains **pollen grains**, not ovules.
- The pollen tube grows through the **style** to reach the **ovary**.

:::tip
After fertilisation: **ovule → seed**, **ovary → fruit**. The ovary holds the ovules, so the fruit holds the seeds.
:::

## Human reproduction

:::remember
- Testes lie in the **scrotum** because sperm formation needs a **lower temperature**.
- **Testosterone** regulates sperm formation and causes the puberty changes in boys.
- The **urethra** carries both **sperms and urine**.
- **Fertilisation** in humans happens in the **oviduct (fallopian tube)**, not in the uterus.
- The embryo is **implanted** in the **uterus** wall.
:::

:::trap
**Vas deferens** is a **male** part. A question may list it with ovary, uterus and fallopian tube and ask which one is not female.
:::

- Seminal vesicles and prostate: their fluid makes sperm transport **easier** and gives **nutrition**.
- Placenta: **villi** on the **embryo's** side, **blood spaces** on the **mother's** side.
- Glucose and oxygen go mother → embryo. Wastes go embryo → mother.

:::remember
Numbers: **one** egg a month; an unfertilised egg lives about **one day**; menstruation lasts about **2 to 8 days**; pregnancy lasts about **nine months**.
:::

- Menstruation happens because the thick uterine lining is **not needed** when the egg is not fertilised.
- Puberty begins as general **body growth slows down**. The changes are gradual and differ from person to person.
- A girl is born with **thousands of immature eggs** already in her ovaries.

## Reproductive health

:::trap
A **copper-T** prevents pregnancy, but it does **not** protect against sexually transmitted diseases. Of these methods, NCERT names only the **condom** as also helping (to some extent) against them.
:::

- **Bacterial** STDs: gonorrhoea, syphilis. **Viral** STDs: warts, HIV-AIDS.
- Oral pills work by changing **hormone balance**, so eggs are not released. Side-effects are possible.
- Copper-T side-effects come from **irritation of the uterus**.
- Surgical blocking: **vas deferens** in males, **fallopian tube** in females.
- **Prenatal sex determination is prohibited by law.** Female foeticide lowers the **child sex ratio**.
